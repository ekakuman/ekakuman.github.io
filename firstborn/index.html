What the firstborn meant · జ్యేష్ఠుని భావన

In Israel the firstborn son held a place of honor and responsibility: a double share of the inheritance, leadership of the family after the father, and the duty to care for the household and set an example for the younger ones.

The double portion · రెట్టింపు భాగంDeuteronomy 21

Even if a man loved one wife more than the other, he could not pass over the true firstborn: “the right of the firstborn is his.” The extra share came with extra responsibility to carry the father's name and look after the family.

Belonging to GodExodus

God called Israel “my son, even my firstborn.” After the Passover, every firstborn of Israel belonged to the LORD and had to be redeemed. Later the Levites were taken in place of the firstborn to serve at the tabernacle.

In the family todayPractical
  • Role and responsibility: the eldest is often expected to lead and to carry more.
  • Setting an example: younger brothers and sisters watch and follow.
  • Leadership and care: supporting parents and meeting household needs.

Birthrights that changed hands · అధికారం మార్చబడటం

Again and again in Scripture, the one born first is not the one who receives the blessing. Sometimes God chose ahead of time; sometimes the firstborn forfeited it.

Esau: a warningHebrews 12

“Behold, I am at the point to die: and what profit shall this birthright do to me?” Esau traded a future inheritance for an immediate appetite. Hebrews calls him a “profane person” and says he later found no place of repentance, though he sought the blessing with tears.

Reuben: unstable as waterGenesis 49

Jacob called Reuben “my might, and the beginning of my strength, the excellency of dignity.” But because of his sin he would “not excel.” Chronicles explains that the birthright went to Joseph, though the leadership came through Judah, from whom came “the chief ruler.”

Christ, firstborn of every creature · సృష్టి అంతటికీ ఆదిసంభూతుడు

Human birthrights can be lost. Christ's cannot. In Colossians, “firstborn” is a title of rank and right, not a claim that he was the first thing created: Paul goes straight on to say that he created all things.

Image, Creator, sustainerColossians 1

He is “the image of the invisible God.” By him all things were created, in heaven and in earth, visible and invisible. He is before all things, and by him all things consist.

  • All things were made by him and for him.
  • Mankind shares a unique privilege: made in God's image .
The firstborn as a royal titlePsalm 89

God says of David's line, “I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth.” David was not his father's first son; “firstborn” there means highest in rank. When God brings his First-begotten into the world, all the angels worship him.

Firstborn from the dead, and our inheritance · మన వారసత్వం

How should we respond?

1 · Recognize Christ's preeminence · క్రీస్తు ప్రథమత్వాన్ని గుర్తించండి

He is first in creation and first in resurrection. Give him first place in your life.

2 · Walk in your inheritance · మీ వారసత్వంలో నడవండి

Live faithfully as a joint-heir with Christ, reflecting his character. Don't trade what is eternal for what is immediate, as Esau did.

3 · Respond in prayer and worship · ప్రార్థన మరియు ఆరాధన

Give thanks for all Christ has done as our Firstborn, and for giving us a share in his inheritance.

  2. Reuben lost his place through one act . How do we guard the inheritance we have been given?
  3. God often chose the younger son. What does that teach about how God's blessing is given?
  4. How does knowing you are a joint-heir with Christ change the way you face suffering?

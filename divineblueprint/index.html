Living the blueprint

Ephesians spends three chapters on the plan and three on daily life. Pick an area of life to see how each part of the blueprint shapes it: the eternal purpose behind it and what it displays before the unseen, what He has provided, who He is making you, where it is heading, and how the end changes it now.

Living the blueprint means living an ordinary life in the light of eternal, invisible realities, knowing that your ordinary life is itself part of how God displays His plan. It works both ways. The unseen shapes the seen: “we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen” (2 Corinthians 4:18). And the seen is on display before the unseen: through the church “now unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places” is “made known… the manifold wisdom of God” (Ephesians 3:10).

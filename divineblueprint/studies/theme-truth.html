Truth and Faithfulness

Overview

A blueprint is only as good as the architect's reliability. Scripture's answer is that God is "the faithful God" (Deuteronomy 7:9) who "cannot lie" and "promised before the world began" (Titus 1:2). Isaiah links this theme directly to the eternal plan: "thy counsels of old are faithfulness and truth" (Isaiah 25:1). Every other theme rests on this one. The promises made in eternity, spoken in history and fulfilled in Christ hold because the One who made them is true. In Christ "all the promises of God … are yea, and … Amen" (2 Corinthians 1:20).

The theme moves in five stages:

  1. God is not a man, that He should lie — His word cannot fail
  2. To the selfsame day — promises kept on time, across centuries
  3. Counsels of old are faithfulness and truth — the prophets trace the plan to God's reliability
  4. Grace and truth came by Jesus Christ — the Amen in person
  5. Faithful and True — the returning King, and words "true and faithful"

Semantic note: One Hebrew root, 'aman, produces "truth" ('emeth), "faithfulness" ('emunah), "believe" (he'emin) and "amen." For Scripture, truth is not only what is accurate but what is reliable, something you can lean your weight on. Faith (theme 31) is the human answer to God's faithfulness: we lean on Him because He holds. Jesus' "Verily, verily" ("Amen, amen") and His title "the Amen" (Revelation 3:14) claim this root for Himself.

Torah

The LORD did as He had spoken — Genesis 21:1–2

1 And the LORD visited Sarah as he had said, and the LORD did unto Sarah as he had spoken. 2 For Sarah conceived, and bare Abraham a son in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken to him.

Meaning: "As he had said … as he had spoken … at the set time": three phrases stressing that God keeps His word exactly, even when it seems impossible (Romans 4:20–21).

His mercy and His truth — Genesis 24:27

27 And he said, Blessed be the LORD God of my master Abraham, who hath not left destitute my master of his mercy and his truth: I being in the way, the LORD led me to the house of my master's brethren.

Meaning: The first pairing of chesed and 'emeth, "mercy and truth." This pair becomes a signature of God's covenant character (Exodus 34:6; Psalm 89:14; John 1:14).

The selfsame day — Exodus 12:40–41

40 Now the sojourning of the children of Israel, who dwelt in Egypt, was four hundred and thirty years. 41 And it came to pass at the end of the four hundred and thirty years, even the selfsame day it came to pass, that all the hosts of the LORD went out from the land of Egypt.

Meaning: The promise of Genesis 15:13–14, kept to the day after four centuries. God's timetable is as reliable as His word (Galatians 4:4).

God is not a man, that He should lie — Numbers 23:19

19 God is not a man, that he should lie; neither the son of man, that he should repent: hath he said, and shall he not do it? or hath he spoken, and shall he not make it good?

Meaning: Spoken by Balaam, a hostile prophet, which makes it all the more striking. God's words cannot be reversed or bought. Hebrews 6:18 draws on the same truth: "it was impossible for God to lie."

The faithful God — Deuteronomy 7:9

9 Know therefore that the LORD thy God, he is God, the faithful God, which keepeth covenant and mercy with them that love him and keep his commandments to a thousand generations;

Meaning: Ha'el hanne'eman, "the God who is reliable." His faithfulness reaches across "a thousand generations," the scale of a plan that spans history.

Prophets (Nevi'im)

Not one thing hath failed — Joshua 23:14

14 And, behold, this day I am going the way of all the earth: and ye know in all your hearts and in all your souls, that not one thing hath failed of all the good things which the LORD your God spake concerning you; all are come to pass unto you, and not one thing hath failed thereof.

Meaning: Joshua's dying testimony. Experience confirms the promise.

Thy counsels of old are faithfulness and truth — Isaiah 25:1

1 O LORD, thou art my God; I will exalt thee, I will praise thy name; for thou hast done wonderful things; thy counsels of old are faithfulness and truth.

Meaning: The key text linking this theme to the blueprint. God's "counsels of old" (theme 2) are made of 'emunah and 'omen: faithfulness and firmness. The plan stands because its Planner is true.

The word of our God shall stand — Isaiah 40:8; 55:10–11

40:8 The grass withereth, the flower fadeth: but the word of our God shall stand for ever.

55:10 For as the rain cometh down, and the snow from heaven, and returneth not thither, but watereth the earth, and maketh it bring forth and bud, that it may give seed to the sower, and bread to the eater: 11 So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it.

Meaning: God's word outlasts everything and always achieves its purpose. Peter applies 40:8 to the gospel (1 Peter 1:24–25).

The God of Amen — Isaiah 65:16

16 That he who blesseth himself in the earth shall bless himself in the God of truth; and he that sweareth in the earth shall swear by the God of truth; because the former troubles are forgotten, and because they are hid from mine eyes.

Meaning: "The God of truth" is literally 'Elohei 'amen, "the God of Amen." This sits right before "new heavens and a new earth" (65:17): the new creation rests on God's faithfulness. Jesus takes the title in Revelation 3:14.

A city of truth — Zechariah 8:3, 8

3 Thus saith the LORD; I am returned unto Zion, and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and Jerusalem shall be called a city of truth; and the mountain of the LORD of hosts the holy mountain.

8 And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and they shall be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.

Meaning: The covenant formula fulfilled "in truth and in righteousness," joining this theme with theme 28. God's dwelling (theme 19) makes His city a place of truth.

Writings (Ketuvim)

Thy faithfulness reacheth unto the clouds — Psalm 36:5

5 Thy mercy, O LORD, is in the heavens; and thy faithfulness reacheth unto the clouds.

Meaning: God's faithfulness is as vast as the sky. It is beyond measuring.

I will not lie unto David — Psalm 89:1–2, 33–35

1 I will sing of the mercies of the LORD for ever: with my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all generations. 2 For I have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy faithfulness shalt thou establish in the very heavens.

33 Nevertheless my lovingkindness will I not utterly take from him, nor suffer my faithfulness to fail. 34 My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips. 35 Once have I sworn by my holiness that I will not lie unto David.

Meaning: Even when David's line sins, God's covenant faithfulness does not fail. The psalm uses "faithfulness" seven times. Its answer is Christ, the Son of David whose throne endures (Luke 1:32–33).

Thy word is settled in heaven — Psalm 119:89–90, 160

89 For ever, O LORD, thy word is settled in heaven. 90 Thy faithfulness is unto all generations: thou hast established the earth, and it abideth.

160 Thy word is true from the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments endureth for ever.

Meaning: The steadiness of creation is evidence of the steadiness of God's word. "From the beginning" can also be read "the sum of thy word is truth."

Great is Thy faithfulness — Lamentations 3:21–23

21 This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope. 22 It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. 23 They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

Meaning: Written in the ruins of Jerusalem. God's faithfulness is confessed when everything else has collapsed.

Speaketh the truth in his heart — Psalm 15:1–2

1 LORD, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill? 2 He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, and speaketh the truth in his heart.

Meaning: Those who dwell with the God of truth become people of truth, inside as well as out.

Understood by books — Daniel 9:2

2 In the first year of his reign I Daniel understood by books the number of the years, whereof the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet, that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations of Jerusalem.

Meaning: Daniel reads Jeremiah 25:11–12 and 29:10 and sees the time is up. That moves him to pray. God's faithfulness to His written word drives Daniel's intercession.

New Testament

Full of grace and truth — John 1:14, 17

14 And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.

17 For the law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.

Meaning: "Grace and truth" translates chesed ve'emeth (Exodus 34:6). The character God proclaimed to Moses now "dwelt among us" in a person.

I am the truth — John 14:6; 17:17; 8:31–32

14:6 Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.

17:17 Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth.

8:31 Then said Jesus to those Jews which believed on him, If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed; 32 And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.

Meaning: Truth is a Person (14:6), a Word (17:17) and a freedom (8:32). The truth that sanctifies and frees is Christ Himself, received through His word.

All the promises … yea, and Amen — 2 Corinthians 1:18–20

18 But as God is true, our word toward you was not yea and nay. 19 For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was preached among you by us, even by me and Silvanus and Timotheus, was not yea and nay, but in him was yea. 20 For all the promises of God in him are yea, and in him Amen, unto the glory of God by us.

Meaning: Christ is God's "Yes" to every promise. Every thread of the blueprint (covenant, Seed, kingdom, rest, inheritance) is confirmed in Him.

God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began — Titus 1:1–2

1 Paul, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of God's elect, and the acknowledging of the truth which is after godliness; 2 In hope of eternal life, which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began;

Meaning: The anchor text linking this theme to "before the foundation." God made a promise within the Godhead before time, and He "cannot lie."

The immutability of His counsel — Hebrews 6:17–19

17 Wherein God, willing more abundantly to shew unto the heirs of promise the immutability of his counsel, confirmed it by an oath: 18 That by two immutable things, in which it was impossible for God to lie, we might have a strong consolation, who have fled for refuge to lay hold upon the hope set before us: 19 Which hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and stedfast, and which entereth into that within the veil;

Meaning: God's "counsel" (boulē, theme 2) is unchangeable, and He backed it with an oath. Two unchangeable things, His word and His oath, anchor our hope.

He abideth faithful — 2 Timothy 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:23–24

2 Timothy 2:13 If we believe not, yet he abideth faithful: he cannot deny himself.

1 Thessalonians 5:23 And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. 24 Faithful is he that calleth you, who also will do it.

Meaning: God's faithfulness does not depend on ours. He will finish what He began (Philippians 1:6).

The Amen, the faithful and true witness — Revelation 3:14; 19:11; 21:5

3:14 And unto the angel of the church of the Laodiceans write; These things saith the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of the creation of God;

19:11 And I saw heaven opened, and behold a white horse; and he that sat upon him was called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he doth judge and make war.

21:5 And he that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he said unto me, Write: for these words are true and faithful.

Meaning: Isaiah 65:16's "God of Amen" is Christ. The returning King is named "Faithful and True," and the promise of the new creation is sealed with "true and faithful."

Principles

  1. The blueprint is as reliable as its Author. God's counsels of old are faithfulness and truth (Isaiah 25:1).
  2. God's word cannot fail. He speaks and does (Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 55:11).
  3. God is faithful to His timing. Promises are kept to the day (Exodus 12:41; Galatians 4:4).
  4. God's faithfulness does not depend on ours. He cannot deny Himself (2 Timothy 2:13).
  5. Truth is a Person. Christ is the truth and God's "Yes" to every promise (John 14:6; 2 Corinthians 1:20).
  6. People of the God of truth become truthful. Truth in the heart, in speech and in community (Psalm 15:2; Ephesians 4:25).

For a teaching session: Read Numbers 23:19, Isaiah 25:1 and 2 Corinthians 1:18–20 together. Ask: what does it mean that the plan is only as strong as the Planner? How does Christ as "the Amen" give us confidence?

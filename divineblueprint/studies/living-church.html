The Divine Blueprint and the Church

Overview

The church is not a human club added to the gospel; it is part of God's eternal purpose. Paul says God's "manifold wisdom" is made known "by the church … according to the eternal purpose which he purposed in Christ Jesus our Lord" (Ephesians 3:10–11). Every earlier theme comes together in it. It is the Bride (theme 21), God's Dwelling (theme 19), a royal Priesthood (theme 30), the place where the Spirit (theme 18) gives gifts, and a preview of all things gathered in Christ (theme 25). The local congregation, imperfect as it is, is where believers live this out together week by week.

This study walks church life through the five groups of the blueprint:

  • A · Purpose: why God builds a church
  • B · Provision: what God supplies to His church
  • C · People: who God shapes us into together
  • D · Goal: a household and holy temple
  • E · Consummation: the great multitude before the throne

Key passage — Ephesians 4:1–7, 11–16

1 I therefore, the prisoner of the Lord, beseech you that ye walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called, 2 With all lowliness and meekness, with longsuffering, forbearing one another in love; 3 Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace. 4 There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling; 5 One Lord, one faith, one baptism, 6 One God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all. 7 But unto every one of us is given grace according to the measure of the gift of Christ.

11 And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; 12 For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ: 13 Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ: 14 That we henceforth be no more children, tossed to and fro, and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the sleight of men, and cunning craftiness, whereby they lie in wait to deceive; 15 But speaking the truth in love, may grow up into him in all things, which is the head, even Christ: 16 From whom the whole body fitly joined together and compacted by that which every joint supplieth, according to the effectual working in the measure of every part, maketh increase of the body unto the edifying of itself in love.

Meaning:

  • vv. 1–3 This is the hinge of Ephesians: the blueprint of chapters 1–3 becomes a "walk." Its first mark is unity, kept by humility, gentleness, patience and love. The Spirit creates unity; we "keep" it.
  • vv. 4–6 Seven "ones": unity is grounded in the one God, Father, Son and Spirit (theme 25).
  • v. 7 Every believer receives grace for ministry. No one is a spectator.
  • vv. 11–12 Leaders are given to "perfect" (equip) the saints, who then do "the work of the ministry."
  • vv. 13–14 The aim is maturity: "the stature of the fulness of Christ," stable rather than tossed about.
  • vv. 15–16 Growth comes through "speaking the truth in love" and through "every joint" doing its part. The body grows "in love."

Interpretive balance: Christian traditions differ on church government (episcopal, presbyterian, congregational), on baptism (infant or believer's, and its mode), and on the meaning of the Lord's Supper. These matter, but Ephesians 4:4–6 names what all share: one body, one Spirit, one hope, one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father.

The five lenses

A · Purpose — why God builds a church

By the church the manifold wisdom of God — Ephesians 3:9–11

9 And to make all men see what is the fellowship of the mystery, which from the beginning of the world hath been hid in God, who created all things by Jesus Christ: 10 To the intent that now unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places might be known by the church the manifold wisdom of God, 11 According to the eternal purpose which he purposed in Christ Jesus our Lord:

Meaning: The church displays God's wisdom even to heavenly powers. It is the mystery (theme 6) made visible, according to the eternal purpose (theme 1).

I will build my church — Matthew 16:18; Exodus 19:5–6

Matthew 16:18 And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

Exodus 19:5 Now therefore, if ye will obey my voice indeed, and keep my covenant, then ye shall be a peculiar treasure unto me above all people: for all the earth is mine: 6 And ye shall be unto me a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation. These are the words which thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel.

Meaning: Christ builds the church, and nothing can overcome it. God's purpose at Sinai, a treasured, priestly, holy people, is renewed in the church (1 Peter 2:9).

The unseen side. "But ye are come unto mount Sion ... and to an innumerable company of angels, to the general assembly and church of the firstborn, which are written in heaven" (Hebrews 12:22–23). Every gathering, however small, joins an unseen assembly in heaven. And through the church God's manifold wisdom is made known "unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places" (Ephesians 3:10).

B · Provision — what God supplies

Diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit — 1 Corinthians 12:4–7, 12–13

4 Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit. 5 And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord. 6 And there are diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all. 7 But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal.

12 For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ. 13 For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit.

Meaning: The Spirit (theme 18) gives every member gifts "to profit withal," for the common good. Spirit, Lord and God appear together: the Trinity supplies the church.

This do in remembrance of me — 1 Corinthians 11:23–26

23 For I have received of the Lord that which also I delivered unto you, That the Lord Jesus the same night in which he was betrayed took bread: 24 And when he had given thanks, he brake it, and said, Take, eat: this is my body, which is broken for you: this do in remembrance of me. 25 After the same manner also he took the cup, when he had supped, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood: this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me. 26 For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye do shew the Lord's death till he come.

Meaning: At the Lord's table the church remembers the Lamb (theme 7) and the new covenant (theme 11), and looks forward "till he come."

C · People — who God shapes us into together

They continued stedfastly — Acts 2:42–47

42 And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers. 43 And fear came upon every soul: and many wonders and signs were done by the apostles. 44 And all that believed were together, and had all things common; 45 And sold their possessions and goods, and parted them to all men, as every man had need. 46 And they, continuing daily with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread from house to house, did eat their meat with gladness and singleness of heart, 47 Praising God, and having favour with all the people. And the Lord added to the church daily such as should be saved.

Meaning: The first church's rhythm: teaching, fellowship, the Lord's table, prayer, generosity, worship, gatherings in homes and growth. These habits form a people.

By this shall all men know — John 13:34–35

34 A new commandment I give unto you, That ye love one another; as I have loved you, that ye also love one another. 35 By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another.

Meaning: Love within the church is its main witness to the world.

Provoke unto love and to good works — Hebrews 10:24–25

24 And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works: 25 Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching.

Meaning: Gathering is not optional extra; it is how we spur one another on, and it matters more as the end approaches.

D · Goal — a household and a holy temple

The household of God — Ephesians 2:19–22

19 Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God; 20 And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone; 21 In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord: 22 In whom ye also are builded together for an habitation of God through the Spirit.

Meaning: Outsiders become family and citizens. Together believers are being built into God's dwelling place (theme 19).

All nations shall flow unto it — Isaiah 2:2–3; Psalm 122:1

Isaiah 2:2 And it shall come to pass in the last days, that the mountain of the LORD's house shall be established in the top of the mountains, and shall be exalted above the hills; and all nations shall flow unto it. 3 And many people shall go and say, Come ye, and let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob; and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths: for out of Zion shall go forth the law, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.

Psalm 122:1 I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the LORD.

Meaning: The goal is a gathering of all nations to learn God's ways, and gladness in going to God's house together.

E · Consummation — the multitude before the throne

A great multitude — Revelation 7:9–10

9 After this I beheld, and, lo, a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues, stood before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands; 10 And cried with a loud voice, saying, Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb.

Meaning: The local church is a preview of this crowd: every nation and language worshipping the Lamb together.

Glory in the church throughout all ages — Ephesians 3:20–21; 5:27

3:20 Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us, 21 Unto him be glory in the church by Christ Jesus throughout all ages, world without end. Amen.

5:27 That he might present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot, or wrinkle, or any such thing; but that it should be holy and without blemish.

Meaning: The church's destiny is glory, presented spotless to Christ (theme 27). Its present flaws are not its final form.

Common struggles, and what the blueprint says

Struggle What the blueprint says Anchor text
Hurt by church or leaders Scripture names controlling leaders (Diotrephes) and calls shepherds to serve, not dominate. God sees, and Christ is the Chief Shepherd. 3 John 9–10; 1 Peter 5:2–4; Ezekiel 34:2–16
Division and factions "Is Christ divided?" Keep the unity of the Spirit with humility. 1 Corinthians 1:10–13; Ephesians 4:2–3
Disagreement on secondary matters Receive one another without quarrelling over disputable matters. Romans 14:1, 19
Favouritism by status, class or background Don't hold the faith "with respect of persons"; all are one in Christ. James 2:1–4; Galatians 3:28
Consumer mindset ("what do I get?") Every member has grace to give; the body grows as each part works. Ephesians 4:7, 16; 1 Peter 4:10
Feeling like an outsider "No more strangers and foreigners"; the church is a household. Ephesians 2:19
Drifting away from gathering Don't forsake assembling, "so much the more" as the day approaches. Hebrews 10:25
Unused gifts Minister your gift as a good steward of God's grace. 1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8

Pastoral note: Some believers have been deeply hurt in churches, through spiritual abuse, betrayal or neglect. Their pain is real, and Scripture takes it seriously (Ezekiel 34). Healing often takes time, safe people and a church that listens. Leaving one unhealthy congregation is not the same as leaving Christ's body, and wise counsel can help someone find a safe community again.

Practices

For yourself

Practice Anchor text
Commit to gathering regularly, even when it's inconvenient. Hebrews 10:25
Discover and use one gift to serve others. 1 Peter 4:10
Pray for your pastors and leaders by name each week. 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17
Speak well of your church; take concerns to the right people, not to gossip. Ephesians 4:29; Matthew 18:15
Come to the Lord's table with self-examination and gratitude. 1 Corinthians 11:26–28

With others

Practice Anchor text
Join or host a small group that meets in homes. Acts 2:46
Welcome a newcomer: learn their name and invite them for a meal. Romans 15:7; Ephesians 2:19
Build friendships across age, background and language. Galatians 3:28; Revelation 7:9

For leaders

Practice Anchor text
Equip members for ministry rather than doing it all. Ephesians 4:11–12
Lead as examples, not as lords. 1 Peter 5:2–3
Hold to the rhythms of Acts 2:42: teaching, fellowship, the Lord's table, prayer. Acts 2:42

For a teaching session (about 40 minutes)

Time Section Read Main point
5 min Opening Ephesians 3:10–11 Ask: "Why does the church exist?" It is part of God's eternal purpose.
8 min A · God's purpose Ephesians 3:9–11; Matthew 16:18 The church displays God's wisdom; Christ builds it and it cannot be overcome.
7 min B · God's provision 1 Corinthians 12:4–7, 12–13; 11:23–26 Gifts for every member; the Lord's table.
10 min C · Formed together Ephesians 4:1–16; Acts 2:42–47 Unity kept by humility; every joint supplying; the first church's rhythms.
5 min D and E · Household and multitude Ephesians 2:19–22; Revelation 7:9–10 Family and temple now; every nation before the Lamb at the end.
5 min Response and prayer John 13:35 Each person chooses one way to serve or welcome. Pray for leaders and for unity.

Discussion questions

  1. What difference does it make that the church is part of God's "eternal purpose" (Ephesians 3:11)?
  2. Ephesians 4:3 says to "keep" the unity of the Spirit. What threatens unity in churches, and what keeps it?
  3. According to Ephesians 4:12, who does "the work of the ministry"? How should that change how we see ourselves?
  4. What is your gift (1 Corinthians 12:7; 1 Peter 4:10), and where could you use it?
  5. Which rhythm of Acts 2:42–47 is strongest in our church? Which is weakest?
  6. How can our church be a "household" for strangers and outsiders (Ephesians 2:19)?
  7. How does the picture of Revelation 7:9 challenge our church today?
  8. Which one practice from this study will you start this week?

చర్చా ప్రశ్నలు

  1. సంఘం దేవుని "నిత్య సంకల్పంలో" (ఎఫెసీయులకు 3:11) భాగమని తెలిసితే ఏమి మారుతుంది?
  2. ఆత్మ ఐక్యతను "కాపాడుకోవాలి" (ఎఫెసీయులకు 4:3). సంఘాలలో ఐక్యతను ఏది బెదిరిస్తుంది, ఏది కాపాడుతుంది?
  3. ఎఫెసీయులకు 4:12 ప్రకారం "పరిచర్య పని" ఎవరు చేస్తారు? ఇది మనల్ని మనం చూసుకునే విధానాన్ని ఎలా మార్చాలి?
  4. మీ వరం ఏమిటి (1 కొరింథీయులకు 12:7; 1 పేతురు 4:10)? దాన్ని ఎక్కడ ఉపయోగించవచ్చు?
  5. అపొస్తలుల కార్యములు 2:42–47లోని ఏ అలవాటు మన సంఘంలో బలంగా ఉంది? ఏది బలహీనంగా ఉంది?
  6. పరదేశులకు, బయటివారికి మన సంఘం ఒక "ఇల్లు"గా (ఎఫెసీయులకు 2:19) ఎలా ఉండగలదు?

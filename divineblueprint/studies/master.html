The Divine Blueprint of God

Overview

God's blueprint has three movements: planned in eternity ("before the foundation of the world"), executed in history ("from the foundation of the world"), and completed in the new creation ("in the dispensation of the fulness of times"). Ephesians 1:3–14 holds all three in one sentence.

Scripture names the plan directly:

  • The eternal purpose — prothesis tōn aiōnōn, "the purpose of the ages" (Ephesians 3:11)
  • The counsel of His will — boulē tou thelēmatos (Ephesians 1:11; Isaiah 46:10)
  • His good pleasure — eudokia, what delights Him (Ephesians 1:5, 9; Luke 12:32)
  • The mystery of His will — hidden, now revealed (Ephesians 1:9; 3:9)
  • The dispensation — oikonomia, God's household administration (Ephesians 1:10; 3:2, 9)

This document maps 33 themes in five groups: the purpose and its Author, the provision, the people, the goal, and the consummation. Each theme shows where it was planned, how it is traced through the Torah, Prophets, Writings and New Testament, and how it ends. Each can then be developed into a full study using the same structure as Before the Foundation of the World: word study, full passages, patterns, principles, practices.

Key text of the series

9 Having made known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he hath purposed in himself: 10 That in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him: 11 In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will: — Ephesians 1:9–11

These three verses hold all five groups of the blueprint:

Group Phrase in Ephesians 1:9–11 Themes
A. The purpose and its Author "the mystery of his will … his good pleasure which he hath purposed in himself"; "the counsel of his own will" 1–6, 28–29: purpose, counsel, love, glory, the Son, the mystery, righteousness and justice, truth and faithfulness
B. The provision "In whom" — Christ as the means 7–12, 30: the Lamb, grace, mercy, redemption, covenant, the Seed, the priesthood
C. The people "we … being predestinated" 13–18, 31: election, sonship, image, life, holiness, the Spirit, faith
D. The goal "have obtained an inheritance" 19–24, 32: dwelling, kingdom, bride, rest, inheritance, book of life, peace
E. Consummation "gather together in one all things in Christ … in the dispensation of the fulness of times" 25–27, 33: all things headed up, new creation, glorification, the final judgment

Why five groups: they follow the questions you would ask of any blueprint, in order: who designed it and why (A), by what means it is carried out (B), whom it is for (C), what they are brought into (D), and how it is finished (E). The grouping is a teaching framework, not a structure Scripture itself prescribes. Some placements are judgment calls; election, for example, is rooted in eternity but is placed with the people because it concerns them.

Companion summaries: Romans 8:29–30 (foreknow → glorified: groups C to E) and 2 Timothy 1:9–10 ("his own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began": groups A, B and D). The full master passage is Ephesians 1:3–14.

Execution timeline

The plan was finished in eternity and is being carried out in ten stages. Each stage puts certain themes into action.

  1. Eternal counsel (before the foundation) — the Father loves the Son; purpose set; the Lamb foreknown; grace given in Christ; a people chosen; eternal life promised. John 17:24; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 1:20; 2 Timothy 1:9; Titus 1:2
  2. Creation — all things made by and for the Son; man made in God's image; the tree of life offered; rest established. Genesis 1–2; Colossians 1:16; Hebrews 4:3
  3. Fall and first promise — man alienated from God's life; the Seed promised; mercy and covering given. Genesis 3:15, 21; Ephesians 4:18
  4. Covenants and the patriarchs — a family chosen; the lamb provided; promises sworn. Genesis 12:1–3; 15; 22:8
  5. Israel, Law and tabernacle — redemption by the blood; a holy nation; God dwells among them by a pattern. Exodus 12; 19:5–6; 25:8–9, 40
  6. Kingdom and Prophets — the Davidic covenant; a temple; the suffering Servant and new covenant announced. 2 Samuel 7:12–16; Isaiah 53; Jeremiah 31:31–34
  7. Incarnation and cross — the Word made flesh; the Lamb slain; redemption accomplished. John 1:14, 29; Galatians 4:4–5; Hebrews 9:26
  8. Resurrection and the Spirit — life released; the Spirit poured out; the church born as God's dwelling. Acts 2; 1 Corinthians 15:45; Ephesians 2:21–22
  9. The church age — the mystery revealed; sons conformed to Christ's image; the bride prepared. Ephesians 3:9–11; Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18
  10. Consummation — Christ returns; kingdom inherited; all things headed up in Christ; New Jerusalem, tree and river of life. Matthew 25:34; Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 21–22

The Bible's structure follows this arc: Genesis 1–2 and Revelation 21–22 mirror each other (tree of life, river, God with man, no curse), and everything between is execution.

Group A — The purpose and its Author

These six themes describe what God intended and why: a purpose rooted in His love, carried out through His Son, aimed at His glory, and hidden until His time.

1. The eternal purpose

God's single, overarching plan for the ages, centered in Christ (prothesis).

  • Planned: "the eternal purpose which he purposed in Christ Jesus" (Ephesians 3:11); "his own purpose and grace … before the world began" (2 Timothy 1:9)
  • Torah: "that I may dwell among them" (Exodus 25:8); the blessing of all nations (Genesis 12:3)
  • Prophets: "I have purposed it, I will also do it" (Isaiah 46:11); "the LORD of hosts hath purposed" (Isaiah 14:24–27)
  • Writings: "the LORD will perfect that which concerneth me" (Psalm 138:8); "no thought can be withholden from thee" (Job 42:2)
  • New Testament: "called according to his purpose" (Romans 8:28); "according to the purpose of him who worketh all things" (Ephesians 1:11)
  • Consummation: "It is done" (Revelation 21:6)

2. The counsel and will of God

God's deliberate, settled decision-making (etsah, boulē) that governs history.

  • Planned: "the counsel of his own will" (Ephesians 1:11)
  • Torah: "God is not a man, that he should lie … hath he said, and shall he not do it?" (Numbers 23:19)
  • Prophets: "My counsel shall stand" (Isaiah 46:10); "wonderful in counsel" (Isaiah 28:29)
  • Writings: "The counsel of the LORD standeth for ever" (Psalm 33:11); "the counsel of the LORD, that shall stand" (Proverbs 19:21)
  • New Testament: "delivered by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God" (Acts 2:23; 4:28); "the immutability of his counsel" (Hebrews 6:17)
  • Consummation: "they shall reign for ever and ever" (Revelation 22:5)

3. The love of God

The motive behind the plan: love that existed within God before creation.

  • Planned: "thou lovedst me before the foundation of the world" (John 17:24); "chosen us in him … in love" (Ephesians 1:4)
  • Torah: "because the LORD loved you" (Deuteronomy 7:8); "he loved the people" (Deuteronomy 33:3)
  • Prophets: "I have loved thee with an everlasting love" (Jeremiah 31:3); "I drew them … with bands of love" (Hosea 11:4)
  • Writings: the Song of Songs; "his banner over me was love" (Song 2:4)
  • New Testament: "God so loved the world" (John 3:16); "God is love" (1 John 4:8–10); nothing can separate us (Romans 8:38–39)
  • Consummation: "loved them, as thou hast loved me" (John 17:23, 26)

4. The glory of God

The goal of the plan: God's worth made visible and shared.

  • Planned: "the glory which I had with thee before the world was" (John 17:5); wisdom "ordained … unto our glory" (1 Corinthians 2:7)
  • Torah: glory fills the tabernacle (Exodus 40:34); "shew me thy glory" (Exodus 33:18)
  • Prophets: "the whole earth is full of his glory" (Isaiah 6:3); "the earth shall be filled with the knowledge of the glory" (Habakkuk 2:14)
  • Writings: "The heavens declare the glory of God" (Psalm 19:1); glory fills Solomon's temple (2 Chronicles 7:1)
  • New Testament: "we beheld his glory" (John 1:14); "to the praise of his glory" (Ephesians 1:6, 12, 14); "changed into the same image from glory to glory" (2 Corinthians 3:18)
  • Consummation: the city has "the glory of God" and the Lamb is its light (Revelation 21:11, 23)

5. The Son — Word and Wisdom

The Person in whom, by whom and for whom the plan exists.

  • Planned: "In the beginning was the Word" (John 1:1); "before all things" (Colossians 1:17)
  • Torah: "And God said" (Genesis 1:3); the Angel of the LORD (Genesis 22:11; Exodus 3:2)
  • Prophets: "whose goings forth have been … from everlasting" (Micah 5:2); "The mighty God, The everlasting Father" (Isaiah 9:6)
  • Writings: Wisdom "set up from everlasting" (Proverbs 8:23); "Thou art my Son" (Psalm 2:7); "what is his son's name?" (Proverbs 30:4)
  • New Testament: "all things were created by him, and for him" (Colossians 1:16); "heir of all things" (Hebrews 1:2)
  • Consummation: "that God may be all in all" (1 Corinthians 15:28)

6. The mystery

The plan's content, hidden in God and revealed in Christ and the church.

  • Planned: "hid in God, who created all things" (Ephesians 3:9); "kept secret since the world began" (Romans 16:25)
  • Torah: "The secret things belong unto the LORD our God" (Deuteronomy 29:29)
  • Prophets: "he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets" (Amos 3:7)
  • Writings: "there is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets" (Daniel 2:28); "It is the glory of God to conceal a thing" (Proverbs 25:2)
  • New Testament: "Christ in you, the hope of glory" (Colossians 1:26–27); Gentiles fellow heirs (Ephesians 3:6); Christ and the church (Ephesians 5:32)
  • Consummation: "the mystery of God should be finished" (Revelation 10:7)

28. Righteousness and justice

The foundation of God's throne, and the reason the plan needed a Lamb: God would be "just, and the justifier."

  • Planned: "Justice and judgment are the habitation of thy throne" (Psalm 89:14); Christ "set forth" as propitiation (Romans 3:25)
  • Torah: "he counted it to him for righteousness" (Genesis 15:6); "Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right?" (Genesis 18:25)
  • Prophets: "THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS" (Jeremiah 23:6); "a just God and a Saviour" (Isaiah 45:21)
  • Writings: "righteousness and peace have kissed each other" (Psalm 85:10); "to bring in everlasting righteousness" (Daniel 9:24)
  • New Testament: "that he might be just, and the justifier" (Romans 3:26); "made the righteousness of God in him" (2 Corinthians 5:21)
  • Consummation: "new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness" (2 Peter 3:13)

29. Truth and faithfulness

The reliability of the Architect: every promise of the blueprint holds because God cannot lie.

  • Planned: eternal life "which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began" (Titus 1:2); "thy counsels of old are faithfulness and truth" (Isaiah 25:1)
  • Torah: "God is not a man, that he should lie" (Numbers 23:19); "the faithful God" (Deuteronomy 7:9)
  • Prophets: "not one thing hath failed" (Joshua 23:14); "the word of our God shall stand for ever" (Isaiah 40:8)
  • Writings: "great is thy faithfulness" (Lamentations 3:23); "for ever, O LORD, thy word is settled in heaven" (Psalm 119:89)
  • New Testament: "grace and truth came by Jesus Christ" (John 1:17); "all the promises of God in him are yea, and in him Amen" (2 Corinthians 1:20)
  • Consummation: "Faithful and True" (Revelation 19:11); "these words are true and faithful" (Revelation 21:5)

Group B — The provision

These six themes show how God provided in advance for everything the plan would need, including the fall. Nothing in redemption was an afterthought.

7. The Lamb

The sacrifice provided by God Himself, foreknown before creation and enthroned at the end.

  • Planned: "a lamb without blemish … foreordained before the foundation of the world" (1 Peter 1:19–20); "the Lamb slain from the foundation" (Revelation 13:8)
  • Torah: "God will provide himself a lamb" (Genesis 22:8); the Passover lamb (Exodus 12:3–13); the daily lambs (Exodus 29:38–42)
  • Prophets: "brought as a lamb to the slaughter" (Isaiah 53:7); "like a lamb or an ox that is brought to the slaughter" (Jeremiah 11:19)
  • Writings: "Sacrifice and offering thou didst not desire … in the volume of the book it is written of me" (Psalm 40:6–7, applied in Hebrews 10:5–7)
  • New Testament: "Behold the Lamb of God" (John 1:29); "Christ our passover is sacrificed for us" (1 Corinthians 5:7); "a Lamb as it had been slain" (Revelation 5:6, 12)
  • Consummation: the marriage supper of the Lamb (Revelation 19:7–9); "the throne of God and of the Lamb" (Revelation 22:1, 3)

8. Grace

God's unearned favor and enabling power (chen, charis), given before we existed.

  • Planned: "grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began" (2 Timothy 1:9); "the praise of the glory of his grace" (Ephesians 1:6)
  • Torah: "Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD" (Genesis 6:8); "I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious" (Exodus 33:19); the priestly blessing (Numbers 6:25)
  • Prophets: "Grace, grace unto it" (Zechariah 4:7); "the spirit of grace and of supplications" (Zechariah 12:10)
  • Writings: "the LORD will give grace and glory" (Psalm 84:11); "he giveth grace unto the lowly" (Proverbs 3:34)
  • New Testament: "full of grace and truth" (John 1:14, 17); "by grace are ye saved through faith" (Ephesians 2:8); "the grace of God that bringeth salvation" (Titus 2:11)
  • Consummation: "in the ages to come he might shew the exceeding riches of his grace" (Ephesians 2:7)

9. Mercy

God's covenant loyalty and compassion (chesed, racham, eleos) toward the undeserving.

  • Planned: "vessels of mercy, which he had afore prepared unto glory" (Romans 9:23); "the mercy of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting" (Psalm 103:17)
  • Torah: "The LORD God, merciful and gracious" (Exodus 34:6–7); the mercy seat (kapporet) over the ark (Exodus 25:17–22)
  • Prophets: "he delighteth in mercy" (Micah 7:18); "I desired mercy, and not sacrifice" (Hosea 6:6); "with everlasting kindness will I have mercy on thee" (Isaiah 54:8)
  • Writings: "his mercy endureth for ever" (Psalm 136, 26 times); "his compassions fail not. They are new every morning" (Lamentations 3:22–23)
  • New Testament: "God, who is rich in mercy" (Ephesians 2:4); "according to his mercy he saved us" (Titus 3:5); "abundant mercy hath begotten us again" (1 Peter 1:3)
  • Consummation: "looking for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life" (Jude 21)

10. Redemption and the blood

Buying back at a price; life given for life.

  • Planned: "redeemed … with the precious blood of Christ … foreordained before the foundation" (1 Peter 1:18–20)
  • Torah: "when I see the blood, I will pass over you" (Exodus 12:13); "the life of the flesh is in the blood" (Leviticus 17:11); the kinsman-redeemer law (Leviticus 25:25)
  • Prophets: "I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name" (Isaiah 43:1); "I will ransom them from the power of the grave" (Hosea 13:14)
  • Writings: "I know that my redeemer liveth" (Job 19:25); Boaz the kinsman-redeemer (Ruth 4); "with him is plenteous redemption" (Psalm 130:7)
  • New Testament: "redemption through his blood" (Ephesians 1:7); "obtained eternal redemption" (Hebrews 9:12)
  • Consummation: "the redemption of our body" (Romans 8:23); "hast redeemed us to God by thy blood" (Revelation 5:9)

11. The everlasting covenant

God binding Himself by oath to carry out the plan.

  • Planned: "the blood of the everlasting covenant" (Hebrews 13:20); eternal life "promised before the world began" (Titus 1:2)
  • Torah: with Noah (Genesis 9:16); with Abraham, "an everlasting covenant" (Genesis 17:7); at Sinai (Exodus 24:8)
  • Prophets: with David, "an everlasting covenant, ordered in all things, and sure" (2 Samuel 23:5); "the sure mercies of David" (Isaiah 55:3); the new covenant (Jeremiah 31:31–34)
  • Writings: "He hath remembered his covenant for ever" (Psalm 105:8); "My covenant shall stand fast with him" (Psalm 89:28)
  • New Testament: "This cup is the new testament in my blood" (Luke 22:20); "mediator of a better covenant" (Hebrews 8:6)
  • Consummation: "they shall be his people, and God himself shall be with them" (Revelation 21:3) — the covenant formula fulfilled

12. The Seed

The promised Offspring through whom the plan reaches the world.

  • Planned: announced at the fall, sent "when the fulness of the time was come" (Galatians 4:4)
  • Torah: "her seed … shall bruise thy head" (Genesis 3:15); "in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed" (Genesis 22:18)
  • Prophets: "I will set up thy seed after thee" (2 Samuel 7:12); "a virgin shall conceive" (Isaiah 7:14); "he shall see his seed" (Isaiah 53:10)
  • Writings: "Thy seed will I establish for ever" (Psalm 89:4); "A seed shall serve him" (Psalm 22:30)
  • New Testament: "to thy seed, which is Christ" (Galatians 3:16); "if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed" (Galatians 3:29)
  • Consummation: "I am the root and the offspring of David" (Revelation 22:16)

30. The priesthood

The Mediator of the plan: a Priest sworn by oath, who offers Himself and ever lives to intercede.

  • Planned: "The LORD hath sworn, and will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever" (Psalm 110:4; Hebrews 7:21)
  • Torah: Melchizedek, "priest of the most high God" (Genesis 14:18); Aaron bearing the names (Exodus 28:12, 29); the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16)
  • Prophets: "a priest upon his throne" (Zechariah 6:13); "made intercession for the transgressors" (Isaiah 53:12)
  • Writings: "Neither is there any daysman betwixt us" (Job 9:33)
  • New Testament: "he ever liveth to make intercession" (Hebrews 7:25); "one mediator between God and men" (1 Timothy 2:5)
  • Consummation: "they shall be priests of God and of Christ" (Revelation 20:6)

Group C — The people

These six themes describe who the plan is for and what God intends to make of them: a chosen family of sons, bearing His image, living by His life, holy like Him, and indwelt by His Spirit.

13. Election and foreknowledge

God knowing and choosing a people for Himself, in Christ.

  • Planned: "chosen us in him before the foundation" (Ephesians 1:4); "whom he did foreknow" (Romans 8:29); "Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father" (1 Peter 1:2)
  • Torah: Abraham, "For I know him" (Genesis 18:19); "the LORD hath chosen thee to be a peculiar people" (Deuteronomy 14:2)
  • Prophets: "Jacob whom I have chosen" (Isaiah 41:8); "mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth" (Isaiah 42:1); "Before I formed thee … I knew thee" (Jeremiah 1:5)
  • Writings: "Blessed is the man whom thou choosest" (Psalm 65:4); "the LORD hath chosen Jacob unto himself" (Psalm 135:4)
  • New Testament: "Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you" (John 15:16); "a chosen generation, a royal priesthood" (1 Peter 2:9)
  • Consummation: "they that are with him are called, and chosen, and faithful" (Revelation 17:14)

14. Sonship and adoption

God's intention to have a family of sons sharing the life and inheritance of His Son.

  • Planned: "predestinated us unto the adoption of children" (Ephesians 1:5); "the firstborn among many brethren" (Romans 8:29)
  • Torah: "Israel is my son, even my firstborn" (Exodus 4:22); "Ye are the children of the LORD your God" (Deuteronomy 14:1)
  • Prophets: "I will be his father, and he shall be my son" (2 Samuel 7:14); "called my son out of Egypt" (Hosea 11:1); "Ye are the sons of the living God" (Hosea 1:10)
  • Writings: "Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee" (Psalm 2:7); "Like as a father pitieth his children" (Psalm 103:13)
  • New Testament: "the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father" (Romans 8:15); "that we might receive the adoption of sons" (Galatians 4:4–6); "now are we the sons of God" (1 John 3:1–2)
  • Consummation: "the manifestation of the sons of God" (Romans 8:19); "I will be his God, and he shall be my son" (Revelation 21:7)

15. Image and likeness

Man created to express God, and restored to the image of the Son.

  • Planned: "predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son" (Romans 8:29)
  • Torah: "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness" (Genesis 1:26–27); the image still in man after the fall (Genesis 9:6)
  • Prophets: on the throne, "the likeness as the appearance of a man" (Ezekiel 1:26)
  • Writings: "crowned him with glory and honour … dominion" (Psalm 8:5–6); "I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness" (Psalm 17:15)
  • New Testament: "the image of the invisible God" (Colossians 1:15); "changed into the same image" (2 Corinthians 3:18); "renewed in knowledge after the image" (Colossians 3:10)
  • Consummation: "we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is" (1 John 3:2); "we shall also bear the image of the heavenly" (1 Corinthians 15:49)

16. The life of God

God's own uncreated life (zōē) offered, lost, regained and consummated. Developed in full in the companion study.

  • Planned: "eternal life, which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began" (Titus 1:2)
  • Torah: the tree of life (Genesis 2:9); "he is thy life" (Deuteronomy 30:20)
  • Prophets: "the fountain of living waters" (Jeremiah 2:13); "I … shall put my spirit in you, and ye shall live" (Ezekiel 37:14)
  • Writings: "with thee is the fountain of life" (Psalm 36:9); "whoso findeth me findeth life" (Proverbs 8:35)
  • New Testament: "as the Father hath life in himself" (John 5:26); "alienated from the life of God" (Ephesians 4:18); "Christ, who is our life" (Colossians 3:4)
  • Consummation: the river and tree of life (Revelation 22:1–2)

17. Holiness

Being set apart to God and sharing His character.

  • Planned: "that we should be holy and without blame before him" (Ephesians 1:4)
  • Torah: "an holy nation" (Exodus 19:6); "Ye shall be holy: for I the LORD your God am holy" (Leviticus 19:2)
  • Prophets: "Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts" (Isaiah 6:3); "The way of holiness" (Isaiah 35:8); "HOLINESS UNTO THE LORD" on the bells (Zechariah 14:20)
  • Writings: "worship the LORD in the beauty of holiness" (Psalm 29:2); "holiness becometh thine house" (Psalm 93:5)
  • New Testament: "Be ye holy; for I am holy" (1 Peter 1:15–16); "partakers of his holiness" (Hebrews 12:10)
  • Consummation: "the holy city, new Jerusalem" (Revelation 21:2); nothing that defiles shall enter (Revelation 21:27)

18. The Spirit

God's own presence given to carry out the plan within His people.

  • Planned: "the eternal Spirit" (Hebrews 9:14); "that holy Spirit of promise" (Ephesians 1:13)
  • Torah: "the Spirit of God moved" (Genesis 1:2); Bezaleel filled with the Spirit to build the tabernacle (Exodus 31:3); "would God that all the LORD's people were prophets" (Numbers 11:29)
  • Prophets: "I will put my spirit within you" (Ezekiel 36:27); "I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh" (Joel 2:28)
  • Writings: "Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created" (Psalm 104:30); "take not thy holy spirit from me" (Psalm 51:11)
  • New Testament: poured out at Pentecost (Acts 2:4, 17); "the earnest of our inheritance" (Ephesians 1:14); "the Spirit of life" (Romans 8:2)
  • Consummation: "the Spirit and the bride say, Come" (Revelation 22:17)

31. Faith

How the people receive the provision: leaning their whole weight on a faithful God.

  • Planned: "by grace are ye saved through faith … it is the gift of God" (Ephesians 2:8); "the faith of God's elect" (Titus 1:1)
  • Torah: "he believed in the LORD" (Genesis 15:6); look and live (Numbers 21:8–9)
  • Prophets: "the just shall live by his faith" (Habakkuk 2:4); "if ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established" (Isaiah 7:9)
  • Writings: "Trust in the LORD with all thine heart" (Proverbs 3:5); "but if not" (Daniel 3:18)
  • New Testament: "whosoever believeth in him" (John 3:16); "faith is the substance of things hoped for" (Hebrews 11:1)
  • Consummation: "receiving the end of your faith, even the salvation of your souls" (1 Peter 1:9)

Group D — The goal

These six themes describe what the plan produces: a home where God and His people dwell together, a kingdom they share, a bride for the Son, an entered rest, a shared inheritance, and names secured in His book.

19. God's dwelling place

God's desire to live with and within His people — from Eden to the New Jerusalem.

  • Planned: "a city which hath foundations, whose builder and maker is God" (Hebrews 11:10); "I go to prepare a place for you" (John 14:2)
  • Torah: Eden; "this is none other but the house of God" (Genesis 28:17); "that I may dwell among them" (Exodus 25:8); glory fills the tabernacle (Exodus 40:34)
  • Prophets: "will God indeed dwell on the earth?" (1 Kings 8:27); "My tabernacle also shall be with them" (Ezekiel 37:27); "The LORD is there" (Ezekiel 48:35)
  • Writings: "the LORD hath chosen Zion; he hath desired it for his habitation" (Psalm 132:13–14); "that I may dwell in the house of the LORD" (Psalm 27:4)
  • New Testament: "the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us" (John 1:14, literally "tabernacled"); "ye are the temple of God" (1 Corinthians 3:16); "an habitation of God through the Spirit" (Ephesians 2:22)
  • Consummation: "the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them" (Revelation 21:3)

20. The kingdom and dominion

God's rule over all things, shared with man through the Son.

  • Planned: "the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world" (Matthew 25:34)
  • Torah: "have dominion" (Genesis 1:28); "The sceptre shall not depart from Judah" (Genesis 49:10); "a kingdom of priests" (Exodus 19:6)
  • Prophets: "thy throne shall be established for ever" (2 Samuel 7:16); "Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end" (Isaiah 9:7)
  • Writings: "Yet have I set my king upon my holy hill of Zion" (Psalm 2:6); "a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed" (Daniel 2:44; 7:14, 27)
  • New Testament: "The kingdom of God is at hand" (Mark 1:15); "translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son" (Colossians 1:13)
  • Consummation: "The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ" (Revelation 11:15)

21. The bride

A counterpart for the Son, prepared through love and sacrifice.

  • Planned: "This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church" (Ephesians 5:32)
  • Torah: Eve formed from Adam's side (Genesis 2:21–24); Rebekah sought for Isaac (Genesis 24)
  • Prophets: "thy Maker is thine husband" (Isaiah 54:5); "as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride" (Isaiah 62:5); "I will betroth thee unto me for ever" (Hosea 2:19)
  • Writings: the Song of Songs; "So shall the king greatly desire thy beauty" (Psalm 45:11); Ruth and Boaz
  • New Testament: "He that hath the bride is the bridegroom" (John 3:29); "a chaste virgin to Christ" (2 Corinthians 11:2); "Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it" (Ephesians 5:25–27)
  • Consummation: "the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready" (Revelation 19:7); "the bride, the Lamb's wife" (Revelation 21:9)

22. Rest

God's finished work, entered and enjoyed with Him.

  • Planned: "the works were finished from the foundation of the world" (Hebrews 4:3)
  • Torah: God rested on the seventh day (Genesis 2:2–3); the Sabbath (Exodus 20:8–11); the Jubilee (Leviticus 25:10); "the rest and … the inheritance" (Deuteronomy 12:9)
  • Prophets: "his rest shall be glorious" (Isaiah 11:10); "ye shall find rest for your souls" (Jeremiah 6:16)
  • Writings: "This is my rest for ever: here will I dwell" (Psalm 132:14); "they should not enter into my rest" (Psalm 95:11)
  • New Testament: "Come unto me … and I will give you rest" (Matthew 11:28–29); "There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God" (Hebrews 4:9)
  • Consummation: "that they may rest from their labours" (Revelation 14:13)

23. Inheritance

What God gives His heirs — and what He receives in them.

  • Planned: "we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated" (Ephesians 1:11)
  • Torah: the land promised (Genesis 13:15); "I am thy part and thine inheritance" (Numbers 18:20); "the LORD's portion is his people" (Deuteronomy 32:9)
  • Prophets: the land divided by lot (Joshua 13–21); "This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD" (Isaiah 54:17)
  • Writings: "The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance" (Psalm 16:5–6); "the meek shall inherit the earth" (Psalm 37:11)
  • New Testament: "heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ" (Romans 8:17); "an inheritance incorruptible" (1 Peter 1:4); "his inheritance in the saints" (Ephesians 1:18)
  • Consummation: "He that overcometh shall inherit all things" (Revelation 21:7)

24. The book of life

The record of those who belong to God, written by Him.

  • Planned: names "written in the book of life from the foundation of the world" (Revelation 17:8); "your names are written in heaven" (Luke 10:20)
  • Torah: "blot me … out of thy book which thou hast written" (Exodus 32:32–33)
  • Prophets: "every one that is written among the living in Jerusalem" (Isaiah 4:3); "a book of remembrance" (Malachi 3:16)
  • Writings: "the book of the living" (Psalm 69:28); "in thy book all my members were written" (Psalm 139:16); "every one that shall be found written in the book" (Daniel 12:1)
  • New Testament: "whose names are in the book of life" (Philippians 4:3); "I will not blot out his name" (Revelation 3:5)
  • Consummation: "they which are written in the Lamb's book of life" (Revelation 21:27)

32. Peace

Shalom: wholeness restored with God, between people, within, and across creation.

  • Planned: "the counsel of peace shall be between them both" (Zechariah 6:13); peace made "through the blood of his cross" (Colossians 1:20)
  • Torah: "The LORD … give thee peace" (Numbers 6:26); "my covenant of peace" (Numbers 25:12)
  • Prophets: "The Prince of Peace" (Isaiah 9:6); "the chastisement of our peace was upon him" (Isaiah 53:5)
  • Writings: "I will both lay me down in peace" (Psalm 4:8); "Pray for the peace of Jerusalem" (Psalm 122:6)
  • New Testament: "he is our peace" (Ephesians 2:14); "we have peace with God" (Romans 5:1)
  • Consummation: "of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end" (Isaiah 9:7)

Group E — Consummation

These three themes describe how the plan ends: everything brought under Christ, a renewed creation, and God's people sharing His glory.

25. All things headed up in Christ

The final aim of the eternal purpose: one Head over a reconciled universe (anakephalaiōsis).

  • Planned: "that in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ" (Ephesians 1:9–10)
  • Torah: "in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed" (Genesis 12:3); "unto him shall the gathering of the people be" (Genesis 49:10)
  • Prophets: "unto me every knee shall bow" (Isaiah 45:23); "the LORD shall be king over all the earth" (Zechariah 14:9)
  • Writings: "All the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the LORD" (Psalm 22:27); "all nations shall serve him" (Psalm 72:11)
  • New Testament: "every knee should bow … every tongue should confess" (Philippians 2:10–11); "by him to reconcile all things unto himself" (Colossians 1:20)
  • Consummation: "that God may be all in all" (1 Corinthians 15:28); every creature in heaven and earth praising (Revelation 5:13)

26. New creation

God finishing what He began: a new man and a new heavens and earth.

  • Planned: "the creature was made subject to vanity … in hope" (Romans 8:20); "new heavens and a new earth" according to His promise (2 Peter 3:13)
  • Torah: the first creation (Genesis 1); the earth renewed after the flood (Genesis 8:22–9:1)
  • Prophets: "I create new heavens and a new earth" (Isaiah 65:17; 66:22); "A new heart also will I give you" (Ezekiel 36:26)
  • Writings: "Create in me a clean heart, O God" (Psalm 51:10, using bara, the creation verb); "as a vesture shalt thou change them" (Psalm 102:25–26)
  • New Testament: "if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature" (2 Corinthians 5:17); "one new man" (Ephesians 2:15)
  • Consummation: "I saw a new heaven and a new earth … Behold, I make all things new" (Revelation 21:1, 5)

27. Glorification

God's people brought into the glory the Son had before the world.

  • Planned: "whom he justified, them he also glorified" (Romans 8:30); wisdom "ordained … unto our glory" (1 Corinthians 2:7)
  • Torah: Moses' face shone after being with God (Exodus 34:29–35)
  • Prophets: "Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee" (Isaiah 60:1)
  • Writings: "they that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament" (Daniel 12:3); "the path of the just is as the shining light" (Proverbs 4:18)
  • New Testament: the transfiguration (Matthew 17:2); "fashioned like unto his glorious body" (Philippians 3:21); "ye also appear with him in glory" (Colossians 3:4)
  • Consummation: "bringing many sons unto glory" (Hebrews 2:10); the city "having the glory of God" (Revelation 21:11)

33. The final judgment

The appointed day when the Judge sets everything right, clearing the way for the new creation.

  • Planned: "he hath appointed a day … by that man whom he hath ordained" (Acts 17:31); "he hath prepared his throne for judgment" (Psalm 9:7)
  • Torah: the flood and the ark (Genesis 6:8); "when I see the blood, I will pass over you" (Exodus 12:13)
  • Prophets: "whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered" (Joel 2:32); the refiner's fire (Malachi 3:2–3)
  • Writings: "the books were opened" (Daniel 7:10); "God shall bring every work into judgment" (Ecclesiastes 12:14)
  • New Testament: "hath committed all judgment unto the Son" (John 5:22); "no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus" (Romans 8:1)
  • Consummation: the great white throne; "death and hell were cast into the lake of fire" (Revelation 20:11–14)

Living the blueprint — teaching studies. These apply the five groups to everyday life. Each has a key passage, the five lenses, common struggles, practices, and a 40-minute teaching outline in English and Telugu. The map's "Living the blueprint" section has all 13 areas and a private personal walk-through.

What it means. Living the blueprint means living an ordinary life in the light of eternal, invisible realities, knowing that your ordinary life is itself part of how God displays His plan. It works in both directions:

  • The unseen shapes the seen. "While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal" (2 Corinthians 4:18). See also Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:27.
  • The seen is on display before the unseen. "To the intent that now unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places might be known by the church the manifold wisdom of God" (Ephesians 3:10). Forgiveness, faithfulness and unity in everyday life show God's wisdom to the unseen powers, and nothing done in the Lord is lost (1 Corinthians 15:58; Revelation 14:13).

This is why Ephesians spends three chapters on the plan (chapters 1–3) and three on the walk (chapters 4–6), joined by 4:1: "walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called."

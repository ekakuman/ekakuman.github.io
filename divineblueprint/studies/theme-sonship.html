Sonship and Adoption

Overview and word study

God's blueprint is not only a kingdom of subjects but a family of sons and daughters who share the life, likeness and inheritance of His Son. Believers were "predestinated … unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself" (Ephesians 1:5), so that the Son "might be the firstborn among many brethren" (Romans 8:29).

Adam is called "the son of God" (Luke 3:38). Israel is God's "firstborn" (Exodus 4:22). David's royal Seed is promised, "I will be his father, and he shall be my son" (2 Samuel 7:14). In Christ, the eternal Son, believers receive both new birth (John 1:12–13) and legal adoption (Galatians 4:5), and cry "Abba, Father" by the Spirit (Romans 8:15).

In Roman law an adopted son received a new name, had old debts cancelled, and inherited fully. Paul's readers would hear huiothesia as total change of status — from slave to son and heir (Galatians 4:7).

Torah

Israel is My son, My firstborn — Exodus 4:22–23

22 And thou shalt say unto Pharaoh, Thus saith the LORD, Israel is my son, even my firstborn: 23 And I say unto thee, Let my son go, that he may serve me: and if thou refuse to let him go, behold, I will slay thy son, even thy firstborn.

Meaning: The exodus is a Father rescuing His son from slavery. "That he may serve me" — sonship and service go together. Hosea 11:1 and Matthew 2:15 apply the theme to Jesus.

As a man doth bear his son — Deuteronomy 1:30–31; 8:5

1:30 The LORD your God which goeth before you, he shall fight for you, according to all that he did for you in Egypt before your eyes; 1:31 And in the wilderness, where thou hast seen how that the LORD thy God bare thee, as a man doth bear his son, in all the way that ye went, until ye came into this place.

8:5 Thou shalt also consider in thine heart, that, as a man chasteneth his son, so the LORD thy God chasteneth thee.

Meaning: Two fatherly actions: carrying and correcting. Both are signs of sonship (Hebrews 12:7).

Ye are the children of the LORD — Deuteronomy 14:1–2

1 Ye are the children of the LORD your God: ye shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness between your eyes for the dead. 2 For thou art an holy people unto the LORD thy God, and the LORD hath chosen thee to be a peculiar people unto himself, above all the nations that are upon the earth.

Meaning: Sonship shapes behaviour: God's children do not adopt pagan mourning rites, because they belong to a living Father.

Is not He thy father? — Deuteronomy 32:6, 18

6 Do ye thus requite the LORD, O foolish people and unwise? is not he thy father that hath bought thee? hath he not made thee, and established thee?

18 Of the Rock that begat thee thou art unmindful, and hast forgotten God that formed thee.

Meaning: God as Father who bought, made, established and "begat" His people — redemption and birth together.

Prophets (Nevi'im)

Thou art our Father — Isaiah 63:16; 64:8

63:16 Doubtless thou art our father, though Abraham be ignorant of us, and Israel acknowledge us not: thou, O LORD, art our father, our redeemer; thy name is from everlasting.

64:8 But now, O LORD, thou art our father; we are the clay, and thou our potter; and we all are the work of thy hand.

Meaning: In exile, when human ancestors could not help, the people appeal to God as Father and Redeemer "from everlasting." The Father is also the Potter, shaping His children.

Thou shalt call Me, My father — Jeremiah 3:19

19 But I said, How shall I put thee among the children, and give thee a pleasant land, a goodly heritage of the hosts of nations? and I said, Thou shalt call me, My father; and shalt not turn away from me.

Meaning: God's longing: to place His people "among the children" with an inheritance, and to hear them call Him "My father." Romans 8:15 fulfils it.

Sons of the living God — Hosea 1:10; 11:1

1:10 Yet the number of the children of Israel shall be as the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured nor numbered; and it shall come to pass, that in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living God.

11:1 When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt.

Meaning: "Not my people" become "sons of the living God." Paul applies this to Gentiles (Romans 9:25–26). Sonship reaches the outsider.

As a man spareth his own son — Malachi 3:16–17

16 Then they that feared the LORD spake often one to another: and the LORD hearkened, and heard it, and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared the LORD, and that thought upon his name. 17 And they shall be mine, saith the LORD of hosts, in that day when I make up my jewels; and I will spare them, as a man spareth his own son that serveth him.

Meaning: God's children are His "jewels" (segullah). He spares them as a father spares his son.

If I be a father, where is mine honour? — Malachi 1:6

6 A son honoureth his father, and a servant his master: if then I be a father, where is mine honour? and if I be a master, where is my fear? saith the LORD of hosts unto you, O priests, that despise my name. And ye say, Wherein have we despised thy name?

Meaning: Sonship calls for honour. A Father deserves His children's respect.

Writings (Ketuvim)

Thou art my Father, my firstborn — Psalm 89:26–27

26 He shall cry unto me, Thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation. 27 Also I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth.

Meaning: The royal Son cries "my father" and is made "firstborn" — a title of rank. Christ is "the firstborn among many brethren" (Romans 8:29).

Like as a father — Psalm 103:13–14

13 Like as a father pitieth his children, so the LORD pitieth them that fear him. 14 For he knoweth our frame; he remembereth that we are dust.

Meaning: A Father who understands His children's weakness and responds with tender compassion.

A father of the fatherless — Psalm 68:5–6; 27:10

68:5 A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, is God in his holy habitation. 68:6 God setteth the solitary in families: he bringeth out those which are bound with chains: but the rebellious dwell in a dry land.

27:10 When my father and my mother forsake me, then the LORD will take me up.

Meaning: God adopts the abandoned and "setteth the solitary in families." Adoption is His character.

Whom the LORD loveth He correcteth — Proverbs 3:11–12

11 My son, despise not the chastening of the LORD; neither be weary of his correction: 12 For whom the LORD loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.

Meaning: Discipline is proof of sonship and delight. Hebrews 12:5–6 quotes it.

New Testament

Power to become the sons of God — John 1:12–13

12 But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name: 13 Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.

Meaning: Sonship by new birth. "Power" is exousia, authority or right. Received through faith, caused by God.

The Spirit of adoption — Romans 8:14–17, 19

14 For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God. 15 For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father. 16 The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God: 17 And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together.

19 For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.

Meaning: The heart of the theme. Led by the Spirit, freed from fear, crying "Abba," assured by the Spirit's witness, and made joint-heirs with Christ. Creation waits for God's sons to be revealed.

No more a servant, but a son — Galatians 4:4–7

4 But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law, 5 To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons. 6 And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father. 7 Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.

Meaning: Two sendings: God sent His Son (to redeem), then the Spirit of His Son (to make sonship real within). Slave becomes son, son becomes heir.

Predestinated unto adoption — Ephesians 1:4–6

4 According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love: 5 Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will, 6 To the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath made us accepted in the beloved.

Meaning: Adoption was the goal of election from eternity. "To himself" — God wanted a family. "Accepted in the beloved" — we are received as the Beloved Son is received.

Not ashamed to call them brethren — Hebrews 2:10–12

10 For it became him, for whom are all things, and by whom are all things, in bringing many sons unto glory, to make the captain of their salvation perfect through sufferings. 11 For both he that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all of one: for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brethren, 12 Saying, I will declare thy name unto my brethren, in the midst of the church will I sing praise unto thee.

Meaning: "Bringing many sons unto glory" summarises the blueprint. The Son calls believers His brothers and sings among them (Psalm 22:22).

God dealeth with you as with sons — Hebrews 12:5–8

5 And ye have forgotten the exhortation which speaketh unto you as unto children, My son, despise not thou the chastening of the Lord, nor faint when thou art rebuked of him: 6 For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth. 7 If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not? 8 But if ye be without chastisement, whereof all are partakers, then are ye bastards, and not sons.

Meaning: Hardship can be fatherly training, not rejection.

My Father, and your Father — John 20:17

17 Jesus saith unto her, Touch me not; for I am not yet ascended to my Father: but go to my brethren, and say unto them, I ascend unto my Father, and your Father; and to my God, and your God.

Meaning: After the resurrection, Jesus calls the disciples "my brethren" for the first time and shares His Father with them.

Now are we the sons of God — 1 John 3:1–3

1 Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not. 2 Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is. 3 And every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as he is pure.

Meaning: Sonship is present ("now") and future ("we shall be like him"). The hope purifies.

He shall be My son — Revelation 21:7

7 He that overcometh shall inherit all things; and I will be his God, and he shall be my son.

Meaning: The covenant promise to David's Seed (2 Samuel 7:14) is extended to every overcomer in the new creation.

Principles

  1. God's goal is a family, not just servants. Adoption was planned before creation (Ephesians 1:5).
  2. Sonship comes through the Son. We share His Father because we are in Him (John 20:17; Galatians 4:4–6).
  3. Sonship is by birth and by adoption. New life from God and full legal status (John 1:12–13; Romans 8:15).
  4. The Spirit makes sonship real. He produces the cry "Abba" and bears witness within (Romans 8:15–16).
  5. Sons are heirs. Everything Christ inherits, believers share (Romans 8:17).
  6. Discipline proves sonship. Hardship can be love (Hebrews 12:7).
  7. Sonship means family resemblance. God's children grow to look like Christ (1 John 3:2–3).

For a teaching session: Read Exodus 4:22–23 and Galatians 4:4–7. Ask how each moves from slavery to sonship.

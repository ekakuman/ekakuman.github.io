Image and Likeness

Overview and word study

Humanity was made to bear God's image: to resemble Him, represent Him, and relate to Him. Sin distorted that image without erasing it (Genesis 9:6; James 3:9). The blueprint's goal is restoration and more — believers are "predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son" (Romans 8:29), and "we shall be like him" (1 John 3:2).

Christ is the true Image: "the image of the invisible God" (Colossians 1:15). The command against making images of God (Exodus 20:4) makes sense here — God has already made His image: first humanity, perfectly the Son.

In the ancient world, kings set up statues (tselem) in conquered lands to show who ruled there. Genesis says God placed living images of Himself on earth — humans — to show His rule (Genesis 1:26–28).

Torah

Let us make man in our image — Genesis 1:26–27

26 And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth. 27 So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.

Meaning: "Created" (bara) appears three times in verse 27, marking humanity's uniqueness. Image is tied to dominion and to relationship ("male and female"). The plural "Let us" hints at fellowship within God, which humanity mirrors.

In his own likeness — Genesis 5:1–3

1 This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, in the likeness of God made he him; 2 Male and female created he them; and blessed them, and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created. 3 And Adam lived an hundred and thirty years, and begat a son in his own likeness, after his image; and called his name Seth:

Meaning: After the fall, Adam passes on "his own likeness." Sonship and image are linked: a son bears his father's image. Humanity now bears Adam's fallen image (1 Corinthians 15:49).

In the image of God made He man — Genesis 9:6

6 Whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed: for in the image of God made he man.

Meaning: After the flood, the image still gives every human life sacred value. The image is damaged, not destroyed.

No graven image — Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 4:15–16

Exodus 20:4 Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth:

Deuteronomy 4:15 Take ye therefore good heed unto yourselves; for ye saw no manner of similitude on the day that the LORD spake unto you in Horeb out of the midst of the fire: 16 Lest ye corrupt yourselves, and make you a graven image, the similitude of any figure, the likeness of male or female,

Meaning: Israel heard God's voice but saw no form. Human-made images reduce God and corrupt worshippers. God reserves the right to make His own image.

Prophets (Nevi'im)

To whom will ye liken God? — Isaiah 40:18, 25

18 To whom then will ye liken God? or what likeness will ye compare unto him?

25 To whom then will ye liken me, or shall I be equal? saith the Holy One.

Meaning: Nothing in creation can represent the Creator. The only adequate image must come from God Himself.

After the figure of a man — Isaiah 44:13–15

13 The carpenter stretcheth out his rule; he marketh it out with a line; he fitteth it with planes, and he marketh it out with the compass, and maketh it after the figure of a man, according to the beauty of a man; that it may remain in the house. 14 He heweth him down cedars, and taketh the cypress and the oak, which he strengtheneth for himself among the trees of the forest: he planteth an ash, and the rain doth nourish it. 15 Then shall it be for a man to burn: for he will take thereof, and warm himself; yea, he kindleth it, and baketh bread; yea, he maketh a god, and worshippeth it; he maketh it a graven image, and falleth down thereto.

Meaning: Satire: people made in God's image make gods in their own image — from the same log they cook with. Image-bearing turned upside down.

The likeness as the appearance of a man — Ezekiel 1:26–28

26 And above the firmament that was over their heads was the likeness of a throne, as the appearance of a sapphire stone: and upon the likeness of the throne was the likeness as the appearance of a man above upon it. 27 And I saw as the colour of amber, as the appearance of fire round about within it, from the appearance of his loins even upward, and from the appearance of his loins even downward, I saw as it were the appearance of fire, and it had brightness round about. 28 As the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud in the day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness round about. This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD. And when I saw it, I fell upon my face, and I heard a voice of one that spake.

Meaning: On God's throne, Ezekiel sees "the likeness as the appearance of a man." The careful layers of "likeness" and "appearance" show reverence. The New Testament reveals this Man: the incarnate Son (Revelation 1:13–16).

Writings (Ketuvim)

Crowned with glory and honour — Psalm 8:3–6

3 When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers, the moon and the stars, which thou hast ordained; 4 What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him? 5 For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels, and hast crowned him with glory and honour. 6 Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet:

Meaning: Genesis 1:26–28 as worship. Image means crowned royalty and dominion. Hebrews 2:6–9 shows it fulfilled in Jesus first.

They that make them are like unto them — Psalm 115:4–8

4 Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men's hands. 5 They have mouths, but they speak not: eyes have they, but they see not: 6 They have ears, but they hear not: noses have they, but they smell not: 7 They have hands, but they handle not: feet have they, but they walk not: neither speak they through their throat. 8 They that make them are like unto them; so is every one that trusteth in them.

Meaning: The law of worship: we become like what we worship. Worshipping idols makes people spiritually deaf, blind and lifeless. The reverse is 2 Corinthians 3:18.

God made man upright — Ecclesiastes 7:29

29 Lo, this only have I found, that God hath made man upright; but they have sought out many inventions.

Meaning: The image was made "upright" (yashar); humanity bent it with its own schemes.

Satisfied with Thy likeness — Psalm 17:15

15 As for me, I will behold thy face in righteousness: I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness.

Meaning: "Likeness" here is temunah, form. David's hope: on waking (resurrection), to see God and be satisfied. Seeing leads to likeness (1 John 3:2).

New Testament

The image of the invisible God — Colossians 1:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4, 6

Colossians 1:15 Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature:

2 Corinthians 4:4 In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.

4:6 For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.

Meaning: Christ is the perfect eikōn. The invisible God is seen "in the face of Jesus Christ." The gospel restores sight to people blinded from seeing that Image.

Conformed to the image of His Son — Romans 8:29

29 For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren.

Meaning: The image is the destination of the blueprint. "Conformed" (symmorphous) means shaped together with — a family likeness.

From glory to glory — 2 Corinthians 3:18

18 But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory, even as by the Spirit of the Lord.

Meaning: The reverse of Psalm 115:8. Beholding Christ, believers are being transformed into His image by the Spirit.

Renewed after the image — Colossians 3:9–10; Ephesians 4:22–24

Colossians 3:9 Lie not one to another, seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds; 10 And have put on the new man, which is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him:

Ephesians 4:22 That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts; 23 And be renewed in the spirit of your mind; 24 And that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness.

Meaning: Genesis 1:27's language returns: "created," "image of him that created him." Salvation is re-creation in God's likeness — knowledge, righteousness and holiness.

Until Christ be formed in you — Galatians 4:19

19 My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you,

Meaning: "Formed" is morphōthē. Ministry aims at Christ's form taking shape in people.

Made after the similitude of God — James 3:9

9 Therewith bless we God, even the Father; and therewith curse we men, which are made after the similitude of God.

Meaning: Every person still bears God's likeness, so cursing people dishonours God.

We shall bear the image of the heavenly — 1 Corinthians 15:47–49

47 The first man is of the earth, earthy: the second man is the Lord from heaven. 48 As is the earthy, such are they also that are earthy: and as is the heavenly, such are they also that are heavenly. 49 And as we have borne the image of the earthy, we shall also bear the image of the heavenly.

Meaning: Two Adams, two images. The resurrection completes the change from Adam's likeness to Christ's.

We shall be like Him — 1 John 3:2

2 Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is.

Meaning: Seeing leads to likeness. Psalm 17:15 fulfilled.

Patterns, principles and practices

Pattern Torah Prophets Writings New Testament
1. Made in God's image "In the image of God created he him" (Genesis 1:27) "I have created him for my glory" (Isaiah 43:7) "Crowned him with glory and honour" (Psalm 8:5) "Made after the similitude of God" (James 3:9)
2. Image distorted, not erased Adam's "own likeness" passed on; image still in man (Genesis 5:3; 9:6) "Walked after vanity, and are become vain" (Jeremiah 2:5) "Sought out many inventions" (Ecclesiastes 7:29) "Changed the glory … into an image" (Romans 1:23)
3. No human-made image of God "No graven image"; "ye saw no manner of similitude" (Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 4:15) "To whom then will ye liken God?" (Isaiah 40:18) "Their idols are … the work of men's hands" (Psalm 115:4) "We ought not to think that the Godhead is like unto gold" (Acts 17:29)
4. We become what we worship The golden calf and a stiff-necked people (Exodus 32:9) "Become vain" (Jeremiah 2:5) "They that make them are like unto them" (Psalm 115:8) "Beholding … are changed into the same image" (2 Corinthians 3:18)
5. The true Image is a Man "Let us make man in our image" (Genesis 1:26) "The likeness as the appearance of a man" on the throne (Ezekiel 1:26) "One like the Son of man" (Daniel 7:13) "The image of the invisible God" (Colossians 1:15)
6. Image restored by new creation "The LORD … will circumcise thine heart" (Deuteronomy 30:6) "A new heart also will I give you" (Ezekiel 36:26) "Create in me a clean heart" (Psalm 51:10) "Renewed … after the image of him that created him" (Colossians 3:10)
7. Image completed when we see Him "Very good" — the original design (Genesis 1:31) — "Satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness" (Psalm 17:15) "We shall be like him" (1 John 3:2); "bear the image of the heavenly" (1 Corinthians 15:49)

Principles

  1. Every person bears God's image. This gives every life dignity (Genesis 9:6; James 3:9).
  2. Christ is the perfect Image. To know what humanity was meant to be, look at Jesus (Colossians 1:15; Hebrews 2:9).
  3. Only God can make His image. Human-made images of God distort Him and us (Exodus 20:4; Psalm 115:8).
  4. We become like what we behold. Worship shapes character (2 Corinthians 3:18).
  5. Salvation restores the image. The new self is created after God's likeness (Ephesians 4:24).
  6. The image will be completed. At Christ's appearing, believers will be like Him (1 John 3:2).
Principle Practice Anchor text
Every person bears the image Speak about and to every person as an image-bearer — no cursing, contempt or mockery. James 3:9–10; Genesis 9:6
Christ the Image Read a Gospel slowly, asking, "What does this show me of God?" John 14:9; Colossians 1:15
Become what you behold Audit what you look at most each day; replace some of it with time beholding Christ. 2 Corinthians 3:18; Psalm 115:8
Image restored Each week, "put off" one old habit and "put on" one Christlike trait. Colossians 3:9–10
Formed in others Pray Galatians 4:19 for those you disciple. Galatians 4:19

For a teaching session: Read Psalm 115:4–8 and 2 Corinthians 3:18 together. Ask what we become by what we worship.

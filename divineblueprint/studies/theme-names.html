The Names of God

A theme study toward understanding the nature of God. Quotations from the KJV.

Why the names matter

The blueprint is God's plan. The names are God revealing Himself as He carries it out. In Scripture a name expresses nature. When God gives a new name, He is showing a new part of who He is. The goal of the plan is that God be known and dwell with His people: "this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God" (John 17:3).

The revelation is progressive. "I appeared unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, by the name of God Almighty, but by my name JEHOVAH was I not known to them" (Exodus 6:3). Each stage of the plan makes more of His name known, until "his name shall be in their foreheads" (Revelation 22:4).

Before the foundation

  • The eternal, holy Name. "the high and lofty One that inhabiteth eternity, whose name is Holy" (Isaiah 57:15).
  • Everlasting. "from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God" (Psalm 90:2).
  • The Name the Son came to declare. "I have declared unto them thy name, and will declare it" (John 17:26).

Consummation

  • "And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads" (Revelation 22:4).
  • "Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them" (Revelation 21:3). YHWH Shammah fulfilled.
  • "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end" (Revelation 22:13).

The names follow the stages of the plan

  1. Conceived: El Olam, Ancient of Days. He is eternal, so He plans before time.
  2. Created: Elohim, El Elyon. He builds the stage and owns it.
  3. Committed: El Shaddai, YHWH. He binds Himself to the plan by covenant.
  4. Carried out: YHWH Yireh, Go'el, the Lamb. He provides the redemption He planned (1 Peter 1:19–20).
  5. Completed: YHWH Shammah, Immanuel, Alpha and Omega. He dwells with His people forever.

The names and Christ's three offices

  • Prophet (God revealing): the Word, Wonderful Counsellor, the Light of the world.
  • Priest (God reconciling): YHWH Yireh, YHWH Mekaddesh, YHWH Tsidkenu, the Lamb.
  • King (God reigning): YHWH Sabaoth, YHWH Nissi, King of kings.

Pattern, principle, practice

Pattern. God reveals a name at a point of crisis: Hagar in the wilderness (El Roi), Abraham on Moriah (Yireh), Israel at bitter Marah (Rapha), the battle at Rephidim (Nissi), Gideon in fear (Shalom). Then He fulfills each name in Christ.

Principle. Your circumstances are where the blueprint is worked out. A need becomes the place where God makes Himself known to you personally. "They that know thy name will put their trust in thee" (Psalm 9:10).

Practice. Live from His names, not from the crisis.

  • When you lack something, pray to YHWH Yireh.
  • When you are sick or broken, pray to YHWH Rapha.
  • When you are anxious, pray to YHWH Shalom.
  • When you feel unseen or alone, pray to El Roi and YHWH Shammah.
  • When you are under attack, pray to YHWH Nissi.
  • When you feel guilty, rest in YHWH Tsidkenu.

Toward the nature of God — next steps

The names open the way to the larger study of God's nature. Themes to develop next:

  • His being: Spirit, eternal, self-existent, unchanging (Malachi 3:6).
  • His attributes: holiness, love, justice, mercy, faithfulness, wisdom, power, knowledge, presence.
  • His triune life: Father, Son and Holy Spirit, one name (Matthew 28:19).
  • His glory: the sum of His names and attributes made visible (Exodus 33:18–19; John 1:14).

Study questions

  1. Which name of God has become most real to you through a specific crisis?
  2. Why did God answer Moses' request to see His glory by proclaiming His name (Exodus 33–34)?
  3. How does Jesus' use of "I AM" change the way we read Exodus 3?
  4. What does it mean to "trust in the name of the LORD" in daily decisions?
  5. How does knowing God by His names shape a spiritual perspective on life on earth?

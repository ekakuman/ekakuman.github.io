The Inheritance

Overview

An inheritance is something planned in advance, held in trust, and passed to heirs by the will of the giver. Scripture uses this picture to describe where God's plan is heading: "Inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world" (Matthew 25:34). In Christ "we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will" (Ephesians 1:11). The inheritance runs in two directions. God's people inherit God's promises, and God's people are themselves God's inheritance (Deuteronomy 32:9; Ephesians 1:18).

The theme moves in five stages:

  1. The promise to Abraham — a land and a seed; Abraham as "heir of the world" (Romans 4:13)
  2. The land divided by lot — each tribe given its portion; Levi's portion is the LORD Himself
  3. The LORD is my portion — the inheritance becomes personal; the nations given to the Son
  4. Heirs of God, joint-heirs with Christ — the Spirit as the down payment
  5. He that overcometh shall inherit all things — the new creation

Semantic note: The Greek klēros ("lot") lies behind the klēronomia word family. This echoes Joshua's division of the land by lot: the inheritance is assigned by God, not earned. Arrabōn is a commercial word for a deposit that guarantees full payment. The Spirit now is God's guarantee of everything to come.

Torah

To give thee this land to inherit it — Genesis 15:7; 17:7–8

15:7 And he said unto him, I am the LORD that brought thee out of Ur of the Chaldees, to give thee this land to inherit it.

17:7 And I will establish my covenant between me and thee and thy seed after thee in their generations for an everlasting covenant, to be a God unto thee, and to thy seed after thee. 8 And I will give unto thee, and to thy seed after thee, the land wherein thou art a stranger, all the land of Canaan, for an everlasting possession; and I will be their God.

Meaning: The inheritance is promised by covenant to Abraham and his Seed. Its heart is "to be a God unto thee." Abraham lived as a stranger in the land, "looking for a city" (Hebrews 11:9–10). Paul widens the promise: Abraham was to be "heir of the world" (Romans 4:13).

I am thy part and thine inheritance — Numbers 18:20

20 And the LORD spake unto Aaron, Thou shalt have no inheritance in their land, neither shalt thou have any part among them: I am thy part and thine inheritance among the children of Israel.

Meaning: The priests received no land because they had something better: God Himself. This is the seed of Psalm 16:5 and of 1 Peter 2:9 (a royal priesthood).

Divided by lot — Numbers 26:53–55; 27:7

26:53 Unto these the land shall be divided for an inheritance according to the number of names. 54 To many thou shalt give the more inheritance, and to few thou shalt give the less inheritance: to every one shall his inheritance be given according to those that were numbered of him. 55 Notwithstanding the land shall be divided by lot: according to the names of the tribes of their fathers they shall inherit.

27:7 The daughters of Zelophehad speak right: thou shalt surely give them a possession of an inheritance among their father's brethren; and thou shalt cause the inheritance of their father to pass unto them.

Meaning: Every name receives a portion, and God decides the lot. The daughters of Zelophehad show that God's inheritance is not lost through any gap in the line.

The LORD's portion is his people — Deuteronomy 32:8–9

8 When the most High divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel. 9 For the LORD's portion is his people; Jacob is the lot of his inheritance.

Meaning: God gives the nations their inheritance, but His own inheritance is a people. The theme runs both ways: we inherit God, and God inherits us (Ephesians 1:18).

Prophets (Nevi'im)

Give me this mountain — Joshua 14:9, 12–13

9 And Moses sware on that day, saying, Surely the land whereon thy feet have trodden shall be thine inheritance, and thy children's for ever, because thou hast wholly followed the LORD my God.

12 Now therefore give me this mountain, whereof the LORD spake in that day; for thou heardest in that day how the Anakims were there, and that the cities were great and fenced: if so be the LORD will be with me, then I shall be able to drive them out, as the LORD said. 13 And Joshua blessed him, and gave unto Hebron Caleb the son of Jephunneh for an inheritance.

Meaning: The inheritance is given, but it must still be possessed by faith. At eighty-five, Caleb claims his promised portion despite giants (compare Hebrews 6:12).

The inheritance of my fathers — 1 Kings 21:2–3

2 And Ahab spake unto Naboth, saying, Give me thy vineyard, that I may have it for a garden of herbs, because it is near unto mine house: and I will give thee for it a better vineyard than it; or, if it seem good unto thee, I will give thee the worth of it in money. 3 And Naboth said to Ahab, The LORD forbid it me, that I should give the inheritance of my fathers unto thee.

Meaning: Naboth would not sell what God had given, even at the cost of his life. Contrast Esau, who sold his birthright for a meal (Hebrews 12:16–17).

The rod of His inheritance — Jeremiah 10:16

16 The portion of Jacob is not like them: for he is the former of all things; and Israel is the rod of his inheritance: The LORD of hosts is his name.

Meaning: Unlike idols, Israel's portion is the Creator of all things, and Israel is His tribe of inheritance.

The heritage of the servants of the LORD — Isaiah 54:17

17 No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is of me, saith the LORD.

Meaning: The inheritance includes protection and a righteousness that comes from God.

I am their inheritance; strangers included — Ezekiel 44:28; 47:22–23

44:28 And it shall be unto them for an inheritance: I am their inheritance: and ye shall give them no possession in Israel: I am their possession.

47:22 And it shall come to pass, that ye shall divide it by lot for an inheritance unto you, and to the strangers that sojourn among you, which shall beget children among you: and they shall be unto you as born in the country among the children of Israel; they shall have inheritance with you among the tribes of Israel. 23 And it shall come to pass, that in what tribe the stranger sojourneth, there shall ye give him his inheritance, saith the Lord GOD.

Meaning: In Ezekiel's restored land, Gentile sojourners receive an inheritance as if native-born. This anticipates Gentiles as "fellowheirs" (Ephesians 3:6).

Writings (Ketuvim)

A goodly heritage — Psalm 16:5–6

5 The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot. 6 The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant places; yea, I have a goodly heritage.

Meaning: David speaks like a Levite: God Himself is his portion. "The lines" are the surveyor's measuring cords from Joshua's land division. Peter reads this psalm as fulfilled in Christ's resurrection (Acts 2:25–31).

The heathen for thine inheritance — Psalm 2:7–8

7 I will declare the decree: the LORD hath said unto me, Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee. 8 Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession.

Meaning: The Son is the primary Heir; the nations are His inheritance. We inherit only by being joined to Him (Hebrews 1:2).

The meek shall inherit the earth — Psalm 37:9, 11, 29

9 For evildoers shall be cut off: but those that wait upon the LORD, they shall inherit the earth.

11 But the meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace.

29 The righteous shall inherit the land, and dwell therein for ever.

Meaning: The inheritance belongs to those who wait, the meek and the righteous, not the grasping. Jesus repeats verse 11 in Matthew 5:5.

My portion for ever — Psalm 73:25–26; Lamentations 3:22–24

Psalm 73:25 Whom have I in heaven but thee? and there is none upon earth that I desire beside thee. 26 My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.

Lamentations 3:22 It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. 23 They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness. 24 The LORD is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.

Meaning: When everything else fails, even in Jerusalem's ruins, God remains the inheritance that cannot be taken away.

An inheritance to children's children — Proverbs 13:22

22 A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children's children: and the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.

Meaning: Wisdom thinks across generations. A godly legacy is both material and spiritual (2 Timothy 1:5).

Stand in thy lot — Daniel 12:13

13 But go thou thy way till the end be: for thou shalt rest, and stand in thy lot at the end of the days.

Meaning: "Lot" (goral) is the inheritance word. Daniel will receive his allotted portion at the resurrection.

New Testament

Inherit the kingdom prepared — Matthew 5:5; 25:34

5:5 Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth.

25:34 Then shall the King say unto them on his right hand, Come, ye blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world:

Meaning: The inheritance was prepared before creation. It is the end goal of the eternal blueprint.

Heirs of God, joint-heirs with Christ — Romans 8:16–17

16 The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God: 17 And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together.

Meaning: Sonship leads to heirship (theme 14). We share Christ's inheritance, and also His sufferings on the way to it.

If a son, then an heir — Galatians 3:29; 4:4–7

3:29 And if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise.

4:4 But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law, 5 To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons. 6 And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father. 7 Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.

Meaning: Through Christ, the Seed, believers of every nation become Abraham's heirs. The Abrahamic promise reaches its full scope.

The earnest of our inheritance — Ephesians 1:11, 13–14, 18

11 In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will:

13 In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise, 14 Which is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory.

18 The eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, and what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,

Meaning: The key passage. The inheritance comes from God's "purpose" and "counsel" (themes 1–2). The Spirit is the deposit that guarantees it. Verse 18 turns it around: the saints are His inheritance (Deuteronomy 32:9).

Appointed heir of all things — Hebrews 1:1–2

1 God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, 2 Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds;

Meaning: The Son through whom all things were made is the Heir of all things. Creation and inheritance meet in Him.

Incorruptible, undefiled, unfading — 1 Peter 1:3–5

3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which according to his abundant mercy hath begotten us again unto a lively hope by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead, 4 To an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away, reserved in heaven for you, 5 Who are kept by the power of God through faith unto salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.

Meaning: Unlike Canaan, which could be defiled and lost, this inheritance is kept for us, and we are kept for it.

Shall inherit all things — Revelation 21:7

7 He that overcometh shall inherit all things; and I will be his God, and he shall be my son.

Meaning: The inheritance completes the covenant formula of Genesis 17:7: "I will be his God." It reaches its full extent: "all things."

Principles

  1. The inheritance was prepared before the world. It comes from God's eternal purpose (Matthew 25:34; Ephesians 1:11).
  2. Christ is the Heir; we inherit in Him. Joined to the Son, we become joint-heirs (Hebrews 1:2; Romans 8:17).
  3. God Himself is the greatest inheritance. Land, kingdom and glory all flow from having God (Psalm 16:5; 73:26).
  4. God treasures His people as His inheritance. We are His portion as He is ours (Deuteronomy 32:9; Ephesians 1:18).
  5. The Spirit guarantees the inheritance. The down payment is already given (Ephesians 1:14).
  6. Faith possesses what grace gives. Like Caleb, believers lay hold of the promises (Joshua 14:12; Hebrews 6:12).
  7. The inheritance is secure. It is kept for us, and we are kept for it (1 Peter 1:4–5).

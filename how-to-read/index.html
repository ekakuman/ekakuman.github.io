How to Read These Studies

ఈ అధ్యయనములను ఎలా చదవాలి · Scripture explaining Scripture

Each study on this site lets the Bible speak for itself. A theme is followed through the whole of Scripture, from the Law to the New Testament, until it arrives at Christ. Then we ask what it shows, what is true, and how we are to live.

Every theme is traced through the whole Bible to Christ, then read in three steps.

Scripture explains Scriptureలేఖనమే లేఖనమును వివరించును

The Bible is its own best commentary. A hard passage is read in light of clearer ones on the same subject, and a teaching is built on the whole counsel of God rather than on one verse alone.

“Which things also we speak, not in the words which man's wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.”1 Corinthians 2:13 (KJV)

Notes on Hebrew, Greek or Telugu words appear where they help, but the passage and Scripture's own use of a word decide its meaning.

The whole Bible, start to finishసంపూర్ణ లేఖనము

Jesus taught from every part of the Scriptures. So each theme is followed through four parts: the Law (Torah), the Prophets, the Writings (Psalms, Proverbs and the other books), and the New Testament. A theme that appears as a seed in Genesis is watched as it grows, is foreshadowed and is finally fulfilled.

“And he said unto them, These are the words which I spake unto you, while I was yet with you, that all things must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me.”Luke 24:44 (KJV)

Three steps in every studyమూడు మెట్లు

Patterns

నమూనాలు

We look closely at what the text says: its setting, movement, repeated words, contrasts, and how the theme connects across the Bible.

What is happening? What is repeated? Where does this theme begin and end?

Principles

సూత్రములు

We state what the passage reveals about God, His purpose and His work, and how it is fulfilled in Christ. These truths hold across every age.

What does this show about God? How is it fulfilled in Christ?

Practice

ఆచరణ

We ask how these truths shape our thinking, worship, homes and witness. God acts first; we respond in faith; what we do on earth matters in heaven.

How does this call us to respond? What is the next faithful step?

Everything points to Christఅన్నియు క్రీస్తును చూపును

God's purpose was set before the foundation of the world and is carried out and completed in His Son. Every study follows its theme until it reaches Him: as Prophet, Priest and King, and in the types and shadows that foretold Him.

“Search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me.”John 5:39 (KJV)

Every verse in its placeసందర్భము ముఖ్యము

A verse means what it means where it stands. Before a verse is used, we ask what came before and after it in the story, who is speaking, to whom, and under which covenant. We also keep apart what Scripture describes (what happened) from what it commands (what we are to do).

Scripture and human knowledgeలేఖనము, మానవ జ్ఞానము

These studies aim to be purely scriptural. Where outside information helps (historical dates, map locations, scholars' views, church traditions, or books outside the Bible), it is clearly marked so that you can always tell the two apart.

What Scripture says

The text itself, quoted from the King James Version, with Telugu alongside. This is the foundation of every study.

Added human knowledge

Shown separately (a marked section, a dashed panel, or a layer you can turn on and off). Helpful, but never the authority.

“Beware lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ.”Colossians 2:8 (KJV)

Where believers differఅభిప్రాయ భేదములు

On some questions sincere believers read Scripture differently. Where that happens, the main views are set out fairly with the passages each one rests on. You are encouraged to weigh them against the Word yourself.

Using each studyప్రతి అధ్యయనమును ఎలా ఉపయోగించాలి

  • Read with your own Bible open. Check every reference and read the verses around it.
  • Start with the overview to see where the theme fits in God's larger purpose, then follow it through the four parts of Scripture.
  • Use the one-page summary image to review or share the whole theme at a glance.
  • Use the slides for group study or teaching.
  • End with practice. Ask what the next faithful step is for you, your home and your church.

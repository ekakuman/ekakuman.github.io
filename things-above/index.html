Two mindsets · రెండు మనస్సులు

“If ye then be risen with Christ, seek those things which are above” . Paul doesn't tell believers to ignore the earth. He tells them where to set the mind, and then shows what each mindset produces.

Set and seek · పెట్టుట మరియు వెదకుటFramework
  • Set is inward: aligning the mind's compass with heaven.
  • Seek is outward: pursuing his kingdom and righteousness first in daily life.
Why a heavenly mindset?Three reasons
  • A new creation: in Christ, old things are passed away.
  • Labor not in vain: the body is mortal, but work done in the Lord lasts.
  • Waiting for redemption: we groan, waiting for the redemption of our body.

Eternity in everyday life · అనుదిన జీవితం

Scripture doesn't divide life into sacred and secular. Ordinary things become “things above” when they are done for the Lord.

Work and careersColossians 3

Do it heartily, as to the Lord, “for ye serve the Lord Christ.” That applies to manual trades, business (honest weights), and management (give servants what is just, knowing you have a Master in heaven).

Creativity and buildingBezaleel

God filled Bezaleel with his Spirit for skilled work in gold, silver, brass, stone and timber. Design and craft can reflect the Creator.

Eating, drinking, everyday pleasuresGratitude

Whatever you do, eat or drink, do all to the glory of God. Received with thanksgiving, ordinary things are sanctified.

Health and the bodyStewardship

The body is the temple of the Holy Ghost, bought with a price. Bodily exercise “profiteth little,” but godliness is profitable for this life and the next. Care for the body, and train the soul more.

Sickness, pain and sufferingWeight of glory

Light affliction, for a moment, works an eternal weight of glory. Tribulation produces patience, experience and hope, and the comfort we receive lets us comfort others.

Education and learningThe renewed mind

The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom. Searching things out is the honor of kings; teaching the next generation carries great weight.

Government and civic lifeMinisters of God

Rulers are “the minister of God to thee for good.” Leading with justice and speaking for those who can't speak for themselves honor God; every believer can pray for those in authority.

Soldiers and serviceIntegrity

John the Baptist told soldiers to do no violence, accuse no one falsely, and be content with their wages. Paul uses the soldier's single-minded focus as a picture of the Christian life.

Money: moving treasure to heavenGenerosity

Lay up treasures in heaven, where nothing corrupts or is stolen. The rich are to be rich in good works, ready to distribute, “laying up in store for themselves a good foundation against the time to come.” God loves a cheerful giver.

Earthly actions with heavenly reward

The judgment seat of Christ · క్రీస్తు తీర్పు సింహాసనము

Every believer will appear before the judgment seat of Christ to receive for the things done in the body . It isn't a trial for sin: there is no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus . It is more like a judge at the games giving out prizes.

Crowns and promises · కిరీటములు

The New Testament names five crowns. Grouping them as “the five crowns” is a teaching tradition, but each one is in the text.

The incorruptible crown · అక్షయమైన కిరీటంSelf-control

Athletes are temperate in all things for a crown that withers; we run for one that does not.

The crown of rejoicing · సంతోష కిరీటంSoul-winning

“What is our hope, or joy, or crown of rejoicing? Are not even ye…?” Paul's crown is the people he brought to Christ.

The crown of righteousness · నీతి కిరీటంLoving his appearing

Laid up for Paul, “and not to me only, but unto all them also that love his appearing.”

The crown of life · జీవ కిరీటంEndurance

Promised to those who endure temptation and to those faithful unto death.

The crown of glory · మహిమ కిరీటంShepherding

For elders who feed the flock willingly, not for gain and not as lords: a crown of glory that fadeth not away.

Promises to the overcomersRevelation 2–3
  • The tree of life: Eden's access restored.
  • Hidden manna and a white stone with a new name: intimate fellowship and personal acceptance.
  • Power over the nations and the morning star: real responsibility in his kingdom (“ruler over many things”), and Christ himself.
  • A pillar in the temple of my God, never to go out again, with God's name written on it.
  • To sit with me in my throne, as he sat down with his Father.

Casting crowns at his feet

Why we receive crownsRevelation 4

The twenty-four elders (many understand them to represent the redeemed) fall down before the throne and cast their crowns before it, saying, “Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power.” Every reward ends as worship.

  • True worship: all glory returns to Jesus.
  • Eternal focus: live today with eternity in view.
  1. Read Colossians 3:5-9 and 3:12-17. Which item on each list is most real in your life right now?
  2. Pick your daily work. What would it look like to do it “as to the Lord” this week ?
  3. Which of your good works might turn out to be wood, hay and stubble because of the motive ?
  4. Jesus promised reward for things done in secret . Why do you think secrecy matters so much to him?
  5. If every crown ends up at his feet , what does that tell us about why we serve?

The journey of divine love · దైవిక ప్రేమ ప్రయాణము

God's love didn't begin at the cross, and it doesn't end with our lives. Scripture traces it from before the foundation of the world into eternity.

Recap: three weeks in the chapter of loveSeries
  • Jesus at Bethany: growing in faith (Mary at his feet), in hope (Lazarus raised), and in love (the anointing).
  • The supremacy of love: greater than gifts and sacrifices; faith first, flowering into the new commandment .
  • The greatness of love: the mark of a great church; living water that flows out, not a Dead Sea that only receives.
Four Greek words for loveWord study

Teachers often distinguish eros (romantic), storge (family), philia (friendship) and agape (self-giving love). The New Testament uses agape and phileo (both appear in Jesus' questions to Peter); eros and storge don't appear as words, though Paul uses a compound of storge in Romans 12:10 (“kindly affectioned”).

The 1 Corinthians 13 standard · ప్రేమ యొక్క శ్రేష్ఠత

Paul describes love with eight things it does not do (love is secure) and six things it does (love is strong). Read them in the KJV , where agape is translated “charity.”

The indwelling Spirit · అంతర్నివాసియైన ఆత్మPentecost

Jesus promised not to leave his disciples comfortless but to send another Comforter. At Pentecost the promise was fulfilled, believers are sealed with the Spirit, and “the love of God is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost.” Our love is not self-produced; it is received.

Gifts without love are nothing1 Cor 13:1-3

The Spirit gives varied gifts to build up the body. But tongues without love are a sounding brass; prophecy, knowledge and mountain-moving faith without love are nothing; even giving everything away, or giving the body to be burned, without love profits nothing.

Temporal and eternal1 Cor 13:8-13

Prophecies fail, tongues cease, knowledge vanishes away: they are partial, for now. Faith, hope and love abide, and the greatest is love.

Walking in agape · అగాపేలో నడుచుట

Laying down our lives1 John 3

Hereby perceive we the love of God, because he laid down his life for us: and we ought to lay down our lives for the brethren. Love is shown “in deed and in truth,” not only in word.

The test of true life1 John 4

Everyone that loveth is born of God. He that loveth not knoweth not God, for God is love. We love him because he first loved us.

Rooted and groundedEphesians 3

Rooted and grounded in love, we grow to comprehend the breadth, length, depth and height of the love of Christ, which passes knowledge. Let love be without dissimulation.

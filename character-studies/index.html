Character Studies in Scripture

బైబిలు పాత్రల అధ్యయనము · What pleased the Lord, and how they point to Christ

Four tiers by coverage

Scripture names roughly 3,000 people, but only about 150 to 170 have enough story or speech to study as characters. This app covers 163 of them, grouped by how much Scripture says about each.

TierCoverageCount
1Multi-chapter or book-length narrative20
2Several chapters or a defining storyline40
3One chapter or a key episode55
4A few verses, but a clear lesson48

Four lenses

  1. Pleased the Lord: what Scripture itself commends, such as faith, obedience, humility, intercession or repentance.
  2. Reflects Christ: where the life is a type or shadow of Jesus (Luke 24:27; Col 2:17; Heb 8:5).
  3. Falls short: where they sinned or failed, and Jesus is the true and better (Heb 3:3; Matt 12:41–42).
  4. Lesson: the principle to draw out for life and teaching.

Every type breaks down somewhere, because only Jesus is without sin (Heb 4:15; 1 Pet 2:22). The failures matter as much as the strengths: each person needed the Saviour they pointed to. Negative examples are marked Warning. A blank lens means Scripture gives nothing clear there, and teaching should not force one.

Read every reference in its chapter before teaching it.

How this is compiled

  • Included: every verse in the Gospels where Jesus speaks of the kingdom of God, the kingdom of heaven, his kingdom or the Father's kingdom; his parables that begin "the kingdom of heaven is like"; his words about himself as King; and the risen Jesus in Acts 1 and Revelation.
  • Left out: verses where others use the word beside Jesus, such as Matthew 3:2, 4:23, 18:1, 20:21; Mark 11:10; Luke 1:33, 8:1, 23:42.
  • Lines in italics say who asked and what. They are summaries, not Scripture.
  • A few verses begin with the narrator's words, such as "He answered and said unto them". Those are kept so no verse is cut.
  • Short parables are quoted whole. For longer ones, the opening and closing verses are given.
Scripture quotations: King James Version (public domain). Companion study: The Kingdom: When, Who, How.

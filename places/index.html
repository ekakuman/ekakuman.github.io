Places of Scriptureలేఖనములలోని స్థలములు

Scripture names the places and the order of travel. Putting those names on a modern map is human work, and some of it is far from certain. This page keeps the two apart, so you can always see which is which.

Scripture · లేఖనముThe place names as the KJV writes them, the order the text gives, the verse, and how the text says they travelled.

Scripture layer: King James Version (public domain) and the Bible Society of India's public-domain Telugu Bible (via godlytalias/Bible-Database). Journeys follow the verses cited; each stop's name was checked against its KJV verse.
Human layer: OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data (CC-BY 4.0), which gathers the proposals of more than seventy atlases, dictionaries and commentaries and scores how far they agree. Coastlines and rivers: Natural Earth (public domain), drawn as they are today; modern reservoirs are left out.

Reading this map well

Scripture shows enough of the unseen to steady our faith, not to satisfy curiosity. Three guardrails keep the picture in proportion.

Worship God alone

Angels are fellow servants. When John fell at an angel’s feet he was told, “See thou do it not … worship God.” We neither pray to angels nor seek them; we pray to the Father through Christ.

Rev. 22:8–9 · Col. 2:18 · Heb. 1:14
The enemy is real, and defeated

The battle is serious but not equal. Christ has already “spoiled principalities and powers,” and “greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.”

Col. 2:15 · 1 John 4:4 · James 4:7
We see in part

“The secret things belong unto the LORD our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us.” The map draws only what is written, and holds it with humility.

Deut. 29:29 · 1 Cor. 13:12 · 2 Cor. 5:7
